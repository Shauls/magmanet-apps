Privacy Policy — Miluim Tracker (מילואים)

Last updated: 2026-10-31 · App: com.magmanet.miluim · Developer: Magmanet

English

Miluim Tracker helps you log your Israeli reserve-duty periods, count the days, track payments and read about your rights. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Service periods you enter (dates, type of service, a free-text unit or place, a combat mark and notes) and payments (kind, amount, date, received or expected, notes) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Estimator inputs (your work status and the income you typed) are stored only on your phone so they are there next time.
  • Notifications. If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled locally on your phone. The notification permission is asked only when you turn them on.

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no server for your data.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used and simple counters, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include dates, units, incomes, amounts or notes. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Links. Tapping a source link opens the official page (for example btl.gov.il) in your browser.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share summary", the text (days per year and payment totals, without unit names or notes) goes only to the app and person you choose.
  • Backup. "Copy backup" in Settings copies your data as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

Not legal or financial advice

Amounts shown are rough estimates from public rules. They are not legal, tax or financial advice. Only Bituach Leumi or the paying body decides what you actually receive. The app is not an official IDF or Bituach Leumi app.

Deleting your data

Delete a period or a payment in the app, or use "Delete all data" in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
