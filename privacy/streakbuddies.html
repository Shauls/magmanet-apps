Privacy Policy — Streak Buddies (שותפים להרגל)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Streak Buddies (שותפים להרגל) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a buddy group (its habit and everyone's daily check-ins) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the friends you invite can keep the streak with you, and only they can see it; you only need a nickname; solo streaks stay on your phone; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your buddy groups, because keeping a habit together is what the app does: the group name, the shared habit (or, in "each their own" groups, your own habit), the day you joined, your daily check-ins, the high fives you give, and short notes you choose to add to a check-in.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your groups.
  • Reports of offensive habit texts or notes (which item was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that item.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

A check-in says only that you did your habit on a day; the app does not measure or collect health or fitness data. The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

"Done / not yet only"

You can turn on "Only done / not yet" when you create or join a group, or later in the group's "My habit & privacy". Then your buddies see only whether you checked in each day: your own habit and your notes are not sent to the server at all (your habit stays on your phone). Turning it on later deletes your habit and notes from the group.

Who can see a group

Only its members (up to 7). A group starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the group name, the habit(s), the other members' nicknames, who checked in on which day, high fives and notes. Nobody else can read a group: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Solo streaks

A solo streak is kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to the server.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they show offline), check-ins waiting to be sent, your solo streaks, your nickname, your own habits and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your buddy groups and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of groups is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your check-ins wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder (off by default) is a local notification scheduled on your phone. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a group (group menu → Leave the group): you are removed from it and your check-ins and high fives in it are deleted. When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a group (creator): the group and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your solo streaks, nickname and habits are removed from the phone.
  • Groups nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the group's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your buddy groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the group name, the habit, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. Nudges and the weekly card are made on your phone. They only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Streak Buddies sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no habits, check-ins, notes or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your buddy groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (asked only when you turn on the daily reminder) and run at startup: showing and re-scheduling the local daily reminder.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy invite".

Children

Streak Buddies is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
