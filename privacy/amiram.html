Privacy Policy — Amir English Prep (אמיר/אמירם באנגלית)

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Amir English Prep is made by Magmanet. It helps you practice for the Amir and Amiram English placement exams. It is not affiliated with NITE (the National Institute for Testing and Evaluation). The short version: your progress stays on your phone.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive your answers, scores, estimated levels, mistakes or vocabulary progress. The app has no sign-up, no accounts, no AI features and no advertising. We never sell or share your data.

Your progress on your phone

  • Practice answers, statistics, mistakes, vocabulary cards (spaced repetition), mock exam results, daily activity and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. You can reset all progress in Settings; uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Mock exam scores and levels are computed on your phone. They are estimates for practice only.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your progress and settings to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. Where you paste it is up to you.

Pronunciation

The "Listen" buttons use your phone's own text-to-speech engine to read English words and sentences aloud. Nothing is recorded, and the app does not send the text anywhere. (Your phone's speech engine is provided by your phone's maker under its own terms.)

Sharing the app

When you tap "Share the app", the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short text and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

Practice needs no internet. The app connects to the internet only for: (1) the update check and (2) the anonymous usage statistics.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "started a mock exam" or "studied vocabulary") and for how long. It never includes your answers, scores, levels, mistakes or anything you typed. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions.

Children

The app is intended for students preparing for higher education. It collects no personal information from anyone.
