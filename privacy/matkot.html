Privacy Policy — Matkot (מטקות)

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Matkot is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Matkot has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your progress (levels, stars, best rallies, total hits), daily rally results and streaks, unlocked paddles and balls, pass-and-play player names and your settings (language, sounds, controls) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Reset progress.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard; the app never uploads it.

Playing with a friend's phone

"Two phones" connects two phones in the same room directly: over the local Wi-Fi network, or, if you turn on "Also use Bluetooth" (Android), over a direct Bluetooth / Wi-Fi Direct link (Google Nearby Connections). The phones exchange only what the game needs: the nickname and emoji avatar each player chose, the beach, and the rally itself (each hit's direction, speed and timing, and when the ball was dropped). Nothing goes through a server, nothing reaches us, and nothing is kept after the game. There is no chat and no free text other than the nickname.

Android asks for location permission before Bluetooth discovery on some versions. It is requested only if you turn on Bluetooth play (after a note that explains why), and the app never reads, stores or sends your location.

Motion sensor

If you choose "Swing the phone" in Settings, the app reads the phone's motion sensor during a rally to notice a swing. The readings are used on the spot and are never stored or sent.

Sharing

"Share" opens your phone's share sheet with a text you can see before sending (your rally length and a link to the app's page). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

The game works entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Matkot sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never nicknames, player names or what you share.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast: the update check, the anonymous usage statistics, and finding a friend's phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and location (only when you turn on Bluetooth play): Android requires them for Bluetooth / Wi-Fi Direct discovery. No location is read.

The app requests no camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

Matkot is a general-audience sports game. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
