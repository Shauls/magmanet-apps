Privacy Policy — Tournament Maker (טורניר)

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · App: com.magmanet.brackets · Developer: Magmanet

English

Tournament Maker runs tournaments between friends: brackets, leagues, groups and stats. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Tournaments: names, players (names, avatar emoji and colours), settings and scores are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Saved player groups and settings (language) are stored on your device.
  • Stats are calculated on the device from your tournaments; nothing is stored elsewhere.

Nothing you enter is sent to us. The app has no account and no server for tournaments. On Android the data is also excluded from Google's automatic app backup. On iPhone, a backup of the whole device that you make to iCloud or a computer may include the app's data, like that of other apps.

What leaves the device

  • What you share. A bracket or table image, or a text summary, goes only to the app, person or place you choose in the share sheet.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens and features used, simple counters such as which format was chosen and how many players, and counts of error types, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include player names, tournament names or scores. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check (Android) asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Copy backup in Settings copies your tournaments, groups and settings as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete a tournament from its menu, and a saved group from its screen. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Images or texts you already shared are not affected.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing about anyone.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues

Français

Tournoi organise des tournois entre amis : tableaux, championnats, poules et statistiques. Tout ce que vous saisissez reste sur votre appareil.

  • Tournois : noms, joueurs (noms, avatars et couleurs), réglages et scores sont enregistrés uniquement dans le stockage privé de l'application. Les groupes enregistrés et les réglages aussi ; les statistiques sont calculées sur l'appareil.
  • Rien ne nous est envoyé. Sur Android, les données sont exclues de la sauvegarde automatique de Google.
  • Ce qui sort de l'appareil : ce que vous partagez, vers la destination choisie ; des statistiques d'usage anonymes (écrans, fonctions, compteurs simples, types d'erreurs et un identifiant d'installation aléatoire ; jamais de noms ni de scores ; désactivables dans Réglages) ; la vérification quotidienne des mises à jour (Android) ; « Copier une sauvegarde » copie vos tournois, groupes et réglages dans le presse-papiers.
  • Suppression : supprimez un tournoi depuis son menu. Désinstaller l'application efface tout.
  • Enfants : l'application ne s'adresse pas spécialement aux enfants et ne collecte rien sur personne.
