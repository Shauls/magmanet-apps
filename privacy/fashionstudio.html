Privacy Policy — Fashion Studio

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Fashion Studio (סטודיו לאופנה) is made by Magmanet. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Fashion Studio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics.

Where your data is stored

Designs, outfits, stars and lookbook "photos" are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are removed when you uninstall the app. Lookbook photos are drawings of the in-app models: the app never uses the camera and never saves pictures of real people. There is no sharing to social networks or other apps.

The grown-ups area offers Copy backup, which copies the app's data as text to the clipboard so you can keep it where you choose. The app never uploads it.

Internet use

The app works entirely offline. Its only network request is the daily update check: it asks the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) for the latest version, sending only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate and stores no identifiers and no IP address. If the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see the IP address). If a newer version exists, a notice appears and the download button is behind the parent gate. You can turn this off in the grown-ups area ("Check for updates automatically").

Permissions

Internet access only, for the update check above. No camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

Children

Fashion Studio is designed for children aged 6–12. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat or social features, no sharing and no links out of the app (except the update download link, which is behind a parent gate), and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Settings are behind a parent gate (press and hold for 3 seconds).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
