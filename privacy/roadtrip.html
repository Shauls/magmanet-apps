Privacy Policy — Road Trip Games

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Road Trip Games (משחקים לדרך / Jeux de route) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Road Trip Games has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

The players' first names (or nicknames) and chosen animals, their points, the current bingo game, the plate hunt, the trip plan (an optional destination name and the expected driving time) and the settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app, and "New trip" clears the points and counters at any time.

Sharing

Nothing leaves the device unless a grown-up passes the parent gate (press and hold for 3 seconds) and presses Share. Two things can be shared, through your phone's own share sheet, to people you choose:

  • A bingo code (RTB-…): it contains only a random number and the card size, so the other phones in the car draw the same game. No names, no points, nothing about the players.
  • The trip card: a picture and a short text with the players' first names, their points and what you played. You see it before you share it.

The app never reads your contacts, never asks for phone numbers and never sends anything by itself. When someone sends you a code, you paste it into the app yourself ("Got a code? Join"); the app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste.

Internet use

Road Trip Games works entirely offline. The only time it goes online is the update check. Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and a grown-up (behind the parent gate) chooses whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Permissions

Road Trip Games' only permission is internet access, used just for the daily update check described above. It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

Road Trip Games is a family app that children play with a grown-up. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no social features, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Settings, every share and the update link are behind a parent gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
