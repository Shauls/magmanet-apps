Privacy Policy — Photo Scavenger Hunt (משימות צילום בקבוצות)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Photo Scavenger Hunt (משימות צילום בקבוצות, Chasse photo en équipes) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a hunt (its tasks, the teams' captions and the judge's verdicts) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the teams you invite can play, and only they can see it; your photos never leave your phone unless you send one yourself; you only need a team name; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your hunts, because playing together is what the app does: the hunt's name, the chosen task pack, its length and start and end time, and its tasks (a ready-made task's number or the text of a task the judge wrote, with its points).
  • Team and judge names (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed) and each team's mascot, shown to the members of the hunt.
  • What teams send for a task: the caption they type and when they sent it. Not the photo.
  • The judge's verdicts (approved or rejected, and any bonus points), cheers (emoji reactions) and reports of offensive captions (which caption was reported, by which anonymous account, used only to hide it).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

Photos are never uploaded. A photo a team takes or picks for a task is copied into the app's private folder on that phone and stays there. The other teams and the judge only see the caption. A team may choose to send a photo to its judge with the "Send the photo to the judge" button, which opens your phone's share sheet; the photo then goes only where you send it. The results card and the team collage are made on your phone and only go where you send them.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a hunt

Only its members (the judge and the teams). A hunt starts with the judge; teams join only with its invite code or link, which the judge chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the tasks, the team list, every team's captions, verdicts, points and cheers. Before the judge starts the hunt, the app shows teams only the number of tasks; that is done on the screen only (the task list is already on their phones). Nobody else can read a hunt: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The judge can remove a team and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. Any member can report an offensive caption; after a few reports it is hidden.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your hunts (so they show offline), captions waiting to be sent, your photos, your name and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your hunts and your name in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your team or judge name only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a hunt is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of hunts is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, what you send waits on the phone and goes out when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Withdraw a task (while it waits for the judge): the caption is deleted from the server.
  • Leave a hunt (hunt menu → Leave hunt): you are removed from it, your cheers and responses are deleted and its photos are deleted from your phone. The captions you sent stay in the hunt for the others.
  • Delete a hunt (judge): the hunt, its tasks, captions, verdicts and cheers are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every hunt, your anonymous account is deleted from the server (DELETE /v1/rooms/me), and your name and photos are removed from the phone.
  • Hunts nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the photos and the local copy, but not your hunts on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the hunt's name and your team name and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your hunts. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the hunt's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site.

Anonymous usage statistics

Photo Scavenger Hunt sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no hunts, tasks, captions, photos or names.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your hunts with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Camera and photos (only when you tap Take photo or From gallery on a task): the system camera or photo picker opens and the one photo you choose is copied into the app's private folder. On iPhone the app asks for camera / photo library access at that moment.

The app has no access to your contacts, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy".

Children

Photo Scavenger Hunt is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. The "Kids' hunt" pack holds safe tasks for an adult-run game with children; an adult should host it and hold the phones.

Changes
