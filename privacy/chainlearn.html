Privacy Policy — Learning Chain (שרשרת תהילים ולימוד)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Learning Chain (שרשרת תהילים ולימוד) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a chain (its dedication and who took and read which part) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can take part too, and only they can see it; you only need a nickname; the texts open on the Sefaria website; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your chains, because dividing the reading together is what the app does: the chain's title and dedication (for example "for the recovery of …" with the name you write), the optional note, the target date, what is divided (Psalms, Mishnayot or your own list, with the part names you write), and for each part who took it and whether they marked it as read.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your chains.
  • Reports of an inappropriate dedication (which chain, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that dedication.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

A dedication usually contains a name (for example the person a recovery chain is for). Write only what the people you invite should see. The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your real name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a chain

Only its members. A chain starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the dedication and note, the other members' nicknames, and who took and read which part. Nobody else can read a chain: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The organizer can remove a member, release a part someone took, and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your chains (so they show offline), actions waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your chains and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a chain is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds (for the live progress), and your list of chains is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your actions wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

Texts on Sefaria

The app contains no texts. When you tap "Open the text on Sefaria", the chapter opens in your browser on www.sefaria.org, a third-party website with its own privacy policy. The app sends Sefaria nothing except the address of the chapter you open.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a chain (chain menu → Leave chain): you are removed from it, the parts you took are released and your "read" marks are deleted. When the organizer leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a chain (organizer): the chain and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every chain, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname is removed from the phone.
  • Chains nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your chains on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the chain's title and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your chains. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite and reminder messages you share contain the chain's title, the parts and nicknames they mention, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The completion card you share is a picture made on your phone. They only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Learning Chain sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no dedications, names, notes or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your chains with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy invite".

Children

Learning Chain is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.

Contact
