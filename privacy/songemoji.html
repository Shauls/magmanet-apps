Privacy Policy — Song Emoji (נחש את השיר)

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Song Emoji is made by Magmanet. The short version: your data stays on your device, unless you choose to join the online leaderboards. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Song Emoji has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

The other exception is the optional online leaderboards (below): if, and only if, you join them, the nickname, city and daily results you choose to publish are sent to the Magmanet leaderboard server.

Where your data is stored

Your daily games, the hints you took, scores, streaks, practice progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Reset progress.

Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard; where you paste it is up to you, the app never uploads it. Share result opens your phone's share sheet with a short text (day number, ✅💡❌, score and the app link) and only goes where you send it.

Online leaderboards (optional, off until you join)

Song Emoji can show public leaderboards for the daily songs (in your city, your region, Israel and the world; today and this week), one for the Hebrew songs and one for the English songs. The first time you open them, the app asks whether you want to take part. If you choose "Not now", nothing is ever sent to the leaderboards.

If you join:

  • What is sent: a nickname you pick, the city you pick from a list (for players outside Israel: the country you pick), the district (region) of that city, and for each day you finish on its own date: the day's points, how much help you took (hints, wrong answers, multiple choice), how many songs you named and how many without help. To check results are plausible, the day number, its language, which songs were played and the points per song are sent too. Archive and practice games are never sent, and neither are the titles you typed.
  • What is not sent: no email, no phone number, no name, no contacts, no location from GPS or the network, no advertising or device identifiers. The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse.
  • Who sees it: your nickname, city and results appear in the public tables, for every Song Emoji player.
  • Where it goes: the Magmanet leaderboard server, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • How long: your profile and results stay on the server until you delete them (see below).
  • Change or delete: Settings → Leaderboards lets you change your nickname or city, stop sending results, or Delete my leaderboard data, which deletes your account, your profile and all your results from the server at once. Uninstalling the app does not delete online data (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so delete it in the app first, or contact us (below) with your nickname and we will remove it.

Party game (local network only)

The party game ("Play nearby") lets phones in the same room play together over the same Wi-Fi, with no server and no internet. One phone hosts and the others join. Only what the game needs travels between those phones, directly over the local network: the first name and avatar each player types or picks, the host's game options and random seed, the answers chosen and the scores. Nothing is sent to Magmanet or anyone else, nothing is stored after the game, and the app only looks for other Song Emoji phones while the party screen is open. Ask a friend shares a riddle's emoji and the app link through your phone's share sheet, only where you send it.

Internet use

Without the leaderboards, Song Emoji works entirely offline; the only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below. With the leaderboards joined, it also connects to the Magmanet leaderboard server, to send your results and load the tables. The app never streams or downloads music, lyrics or pictures.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed; the server stores no identifiers and no IP address. If the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). You can turn this off in Settings.

Anonymous usage statistics

Song Emoji sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID made by the app, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used, for how long, which hints were taken, and points per song or day). Never the titles you type, and no leaderboard nickname or city. On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored; raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app.

This is on by default. Turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"); that stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check, the anonymous usage statistics and the optional leaderboards.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast (Android), local network (iPhone): only for the party game, to find and join phones on the same Wi-Fi.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or notification permissions.

Children

Song Emoji is a general-audience quiz. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information unless a player chooses to join the leaderboards, which are not meant for children.

Changes and contact
