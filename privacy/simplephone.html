Privacy Policy — Simple Phone (טלפון פשוט)

Last updated: 2026-11-05 · App: com.magmanet.simplephone · Developer: Magmanet

English

Simple Phone is a large-button home screen, set up by a family member. Everything it saves stays on the phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • People on the tiles (name, phone number, an optional photo, whether to show a WhatsApp tile) are stored only in the app's private storage on the phone. They are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.
  • The emergency number the family chose, the chosen app tiles and the display settings, likewise.

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no server for this data.

Contacts, photos and the app list

  • Contacts. The app has no contacts permission. "Choose from the phone's contacts" opens the system's own picker, and only the entry you tap is copied into the app.
  • Photos. You choose or take a photo with the system's screens. It is copied into the app's private storage.
  • Other apps (Android). The app reads the list of installed apps to offer app tiles. The list stays on the phone.
  • Battery. The level is read to show it on screen. It stays on the phone.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used, which kinds of tile are tapped, how many people and apps are set up, counts of errors, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include names, phone numbers, photos or which apps were chosen. A family member can turn them off in Family setup > More settings.
  • On Android, each request also carries the app version, Android version, phone manufacturer and model, language and installer. On iOS it carries the app version, iOS version, language and installer, and no device model.
  • The update check (Android) asks our website whether a newer version exists, at most once a day when Family setup is opened. It can be turned off in More settings, and the home screen never shows an update notice.
  • Calls and chats. A tile hands the number to the phone's dialer, or to WhatsApp through its public link (https://wa.me/...). What happens then is covered by those apps' own policies.
  • Backup. "Copy backup" copies names, numbers and settings as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

The emergency tile

The tile only opens the phone's dialer with the number filled in. The person still has to press call. The app does not call anyone by itself, does not send help and is not an emergency service.

Deleting your data

Delete a person in Family setup (their photo is deleted too). Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Turning statistics off deletes the random ID and any unsent statistics from the phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues

  • אנשי קשר: לאפליקציה אין הרשאה לאנשי הקשר. הבחירה נעשית ברשימה של הטלפון, ורק הרשומה שמקישים עליה מועתקת.

  • אפליקציות אחרות (אנדרואיד): הרשימה נקראת כדי להציע כפתורים ונשארת בטלפון. סוללה: האחוז נקרא להצגה ונשאר בטלפון.
  • מה יוצא מהטלפון: סטטיסטיקת שימוש אנונימית (מסכים, סוגי כפתורים שהוקשו, כמה אנשים ואפליקציות הוגדרו, ספירת שגיאות ומזהה אקראי), בלי שמות, מספרים, תמונות או שמות האפליקציות שנבחרו. אפשר לכבות בהגדרות המשפחה > הגדרות נוספות. בכל בקשה נשלחות גם גרסת האפליקציה ומערכת ההפעלה, השפה ומקור ההתקנה, ובאנדרואיד גם יצרן ודגם המכשיר. בדיקת עדכון (אנדרואיד) רצה לכל היותר פעם ביום כשפותחים את הגדרות המשפחה, ומסך הבית לא מציג הודעות עדכון.

  • מחיקה: מחיקת אדם בהגדרות המשפחה מוחקת גם את התמונה. הסרת האפליקציה מוחקת הכול.
