Privacy Policy — Gym Trainer

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Gym Trainer is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device; the app only sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

Gym Trainer has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing we collect is anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

To provide some features, the app contacts these third-party services directly from your device:

  • YouTube, TikTok and Vimeo oEmbed services (youtube.com, img.youtube.com, tiktok.com, vimeo.com). When you save a video from one of these platforms, the app sends that video's public link to get its title and thumbnail. Opening a video hands its link to the platform's own app or your browser. Privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy, https://vimeo.com/privacy
  • The update check. Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. We don't receive or store these requests.

Anonymous usage statistics

Gym Trainer sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Camera and microphone: to record a video of your attempt inside the app. Videos are saved on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Photos and videos: to pick a video you choose as proof. It stays on your device.
  • Opening links: if you choose to open a link, it opens in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
