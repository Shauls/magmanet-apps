Privacy Policy — Jigsaw (פאזלים)

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Jigsaw is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data and your photos stay on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off. They never include your photos.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Jigsaw has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Your photos

You can choose one of your own photos to turn into a puzzle. The app opens your phone's photo picker, and only the photo you pick is read. It is processed only on your device: the app keeps a private copy in its own folder so you can continue the puzzle later, cuts it into pieces on the phone, and shows it to you. The photo is never uploaded, never sent to Magmanet or anyone else, and never included in usage statistics, backups the app makes or shared results. The private copy is deleted when you finish the puzzle, delete it from "My puzzles", reset progress, or uninstall the app.

Where your data is stored

Your puzzles in progress (piece positions, times), finished pictures, best times, daily results and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Reset progress.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your progress and settings (not your photos) as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sharing results

When you tap "Share result" after a daily puzzle, the app opens your phone's share sheet with a text you can see before sending: the puzzle number, the number of pieces, your time and a link to the app's page. It contains nothing about you and no picture. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

The game works entirely offline. All pictures are drawn by the app on the phone. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Jigsaw sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, and the number of pieces of a puzzle started or finished). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent — never your photos, never which photo you used.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics described above.
  • Photos (photo picker): only when you choose "Your own photo", and only for the photo you pick. On Android the system photo picker is used, so the app gets no access to the rest of your gallery. On iPhone the app asks for photo library access with an explanation.

The app requests no notification, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

Children

Jigsaw is a general-audience puzzle game. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
