Privacy Policy — Board Duel (מלך הלוח)

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Board Duel (מלך הלוח) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, unless you choose to play games with friends online; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Board Duel has no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

The other exception is the optional games with friends (below): if, and only if, you use them, the nickname you pick and the moves of those games are sent to the Magmanet server.

Where your data is stored

Your settings, your statistics (games won, drawn and lost per game and level) and an unfinished game against the computer or between two players on one phone are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Copy PGN / Copy game copies the moves of a game to your clipboard. Where you paste them is up to you; the app never uploads them.

Games with friends (optional, off until you use them)

"Games with friends" lets you play chess or checkers with a friend far away, one move at a time: you make a move, your friend answers whenever they can, and you can have several games going. Nothing is sent until you open this screen and pick a nickname.

  • What is sent: a nickname you pick (2–16 characters, shown only to the friends you play with; no real name needed), the games you start or join (chess or checkers, colours, the time per move you chose), every move you make, draw offers, resignations, and the six fixed reactions (👍 😂 😮 🤔 🔥 🤝) you tap. There is no chat and no free text other than the nickname.
  • What is not sent: no email, no phone number, no name, no contacts, no location, no advertising or device identifiers. The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse.
  • Who sees it: only the friend you play each game with sees your nickname and that game. Nobody else can read your games; there are no public lists or rankings. A friend joins a game with the invite code or link that you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp); share it only with the person you want to play.
  • Where it goes: the Magmanet server, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The server keeps no access log with your IP address for these requests.
  • Notifications: there are none. The app asks the server for new moves when you open it, when you come back to it, and every few seconds while a game is on screen. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for new moves"); then you see new moves when you open a game.
  • How long: unused invites expire after 7 days. Your games stay on the server while they are in progress and are deleted one year after they end. Your nickname stays until you delete it.
  • Change or delete: Settings → Games with friends lets you change your nickname or Delete my online data, which deletes your account, your nickname and all your games with friends from the server at once (also from your friends' lists, since a game belongs to both players). Uninstalling the app does not delete online data (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so delete it in the app first, or contact us (below) with your nickname and we will remove it.

Internet use

Without games with friends, Board Duel works entirely offline, including the computer opponent, which runs on your phone; the only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below. With games with friends, it also connects to the Magmanet server to send your moves and load your games. The Android "internet" permission is needed for that and for any network connection, including the direct local-network connection between phones in Play nearby.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

An invite link opens a page on the Magmanet apps site (GitHub Pages) that offers to open the game in the app or to download it; the invite code is in the part of the link after "#", which browsers don't send to the site.

Anonymous usage statistics

Board Duel sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a chess game against the hard computer ended). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: no game moves and no games-with-friends nickname.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Play nearby

When you play with a friend's phone nearby, the phones talk to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi network or hotspot, or over Bluetooth if you turn that option on. Each phone shares the display name and emoji avatar you choose, and the game itself: the moves, the clocks, draw offers and the result. Other phones that joined the same room to watch see the same game. This goes only between the phones in the room; it never passes through a server and Magmanet never receives it. There is no chat.

Permissions

  • Internet and Wi-Fi state: games with friends, Play nearby on the local network (finding and connecting to the other phones), the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices (Android): only for the optional "Also use Bluetooth" switch in Play nearby, which is off by default. When you turn it on, the app first explains why, then Android asks for permission to find and connect to nearby devices. If you refuse, Play nearby keeps working over Wi-Fi.
  • Location: Android requires the location permission for finding nearby devices over Bluetooth on some versions, and asks for it together with the Bluetooth permission. Board Duel never reads, stores or sends your location.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and sends no notifications.

Children

Board Duel is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. Games with friends are only for players who choose to use them.
