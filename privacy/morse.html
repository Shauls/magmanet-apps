Privacy Policy — Morse Code (קוד מורס)

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Morse Code is made by Magmanet. It teaches Morse code. The short version: your progress stays on your phone. The app has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising, and we never sell or share your data.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive your answers, your scores per character, or anything you type, send with the key or translate.

Data on your phone

  • Progress (Koch level for each alphabet, how often each character was heard and answered right, your streak, session count and best rhythm score) and settings (language, tone, speeds, sound, flash and vibration) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Settings → Reset progress deletes the progress; uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your settings and progress to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. Where you paste it is up to you.
  • The Morse tone is generated on the phone and written to the app's temporary folder so the audio player can play it. It contains only the tone.

Audio, flash and vibration

The app plays a tone it generates itself, flashes the screen and can vibrate. Nothing is recorded: the app has no microphone or camera permission and does not use the camera flashlight.

Sharing

"Share the app" opens your phone's share sheet with a short text and a link to the app's page. "Copy" in the translator copies the Morse to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

All exercises are built in and work offline. The app connects to the internet only for (1) the update check and (2) the anonymous usage statistics.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "finished a Koch lesson" with its score, or "shared the app") and for how long. It never includes your answers, what you keyed or anything you typed or translated. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions. Vibration uses the system's haptic feedback, which needs no permission.

Children

The app is intended for adults, teens, scouts and hobbyists. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
