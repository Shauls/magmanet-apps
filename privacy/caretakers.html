Privacy Policy — Caretakers (דואגים לאבא ולאמא)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Caretakers (דואגים לאבא ולאמא) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: what your family writes in a circle is stored on the Magmanet server so that the relatives you invite can coordinate care together, and only they can see it; you only need a nickname; there are no payments in the app; health notes are never used for anything except showing them to your circle; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your family circles, because coordinating care together is what the app does:
  • the circle's name, who it is for (e.g. "Mom") and its currency;
  • visit days and who claimed them; tasks (what, type, due date, who took it, who did it); appointments (what, when, where, who is driving, and the notes written after);
  • check-ins: who wrote them, whether they visited, a mood and a note;
  • the medication list (name, the text copied from the prescription, a note);
  • expenses (what for, the amount, which member paid) and notes that money was handed over between members;
  • the emergency info card (any of: full name, year of birth, blood type, allergies, medical conditions, family doctor and phone, health fund or insurance, preferred hospital, emergency contacts, important notes).
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your circles.
  • Reports of offensive content (which item was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that item.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for your email address, phone number, real name, contacts, location or any payment details. Expenses are notes only; no money moves through the app. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Health information

Much of what families write is about someone's health: moods, notes after a visit or a doctor's appointment, medications, allergies and conditions. This information is:

  • typed by members of the circle, about the person they care for, by their own choice (every field is optional);
  • shown only to the members of that circle;
  • never used for analytics, advertising, research, profiling or any purpose other than showing it to the circle;
  • never sent to third parties (not to Google, health funds, hospitals, pharmacies or anyone else);
  • deletable at any time (see below).

Please write only what your family needs, and ask the person you care for what they are comfortable sharing with the family. The app is not a medical device, gives no medical advice and does not replace a doctor, nurse or pharmacist. The medication list is for reference only.

Who can see a circle

Only its members. A circle starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example in a family chat). Members see everything in the circle and each other's nicknames. Nobody else can read a circle: the server refuses it to anyone who is not a member, and there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your circles (so they show offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname, the list of reminders the app scheduled and your settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your circles and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

While a circle is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of circles is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, what you add waits on the phone and is sent when you are back online.

Reminders

When you take a visit day, a task with a due date or an appointment drive, the app can show a local notification (the evening before a visit or task, at an hour you choose, and two hours before a drive). Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself; no push service is used and nothing about them is sent to any server. Turn them off in Settings.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete an item you wrote (a task, appointment, check-in, medication, expense or hand-over note; the circle's creator can delete any item): it is deleted for everyone. The emergency card can be cleared or deleted the same way.
  • Leave a circle (circle menu → Leave the circle): you are removed from it and your claims (visit days, tasks, drives) are released; items you wrote stay in the circle unless you delete them first. When the creator leaves, the longest-standing member takes over.
  • Delete a circle (creator): the circle and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every circle, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname is removed from the phone.
  • Circles nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your circles on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the circle's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your circles. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message, the week's visit schedule and the thank-you message are made on your phone, and the emergency card is copied on your phone only when you tap "Copy". They only go where you send them, through the app you choose. The link to the Meds on Time app opens its public store page in your browser; no data passes between the apps.

Anonymous usage statistics

Caretakers sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no circle names, notes, health information, medications, expenses or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your circles with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (and starting after a reboot): the local reminders for visits, tasks and drives you took.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy" (the invite or the emergency card).

Children

Caretakers is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes
