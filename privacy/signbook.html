Privacy Policy — Sign Book (ספר ברכות)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Sign Book (ספר ברכות) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your books are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can sign and read them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your books, because signing together is what the app does: the title, the occasion, the name on the cover, the cover design and the opening time.
  • Your pages: the message, the doodle (as small vector strokes), stickers, paper and ink colour, and which member wrote each page. Hearts you leave on pages and reports you send.
  • Your nickname in each book (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the book's members.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a book

Only its members. A book starts with its organizer; others join only with the book's invite link or code, which members choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the pages and the other members' nicknames. Before the opening time, the person the book is for receives from the server only the number of pages others wrote, not their content. Nobody outside the book can read it: there are no public books, no search and no profiles. The organizer can remove a member, delete pages and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your books (so they open offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your books, pages and nicknames, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete a page: the writer or the organizer can delete it.
  • Leave the book (book menu): you are removed from it; your pages stay for the other members. When the last member leaves, the book is deleted from the server.
  • Delete the book (organizer): the book and all its pages are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every book and your anonymous account is erased.
  • Books nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted pages are kept for up to 30 days so syncing works, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your books on the server, so leave them or use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below).

Reports

Every page written by someone else has a Report action. A page reported by several members is hidden from everyone.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your books. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo); the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed. If the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). You can turn this off in Settings.

Invite, reminder and "send the book" messages contain the book's title, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. PDFs and images of a book are made on your phone. All of these only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Sign Book sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party: the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model, your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used, for example that a book was created or a page added). No book titles, pages or nicknames are sent with the statistics.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. This is on by default; turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"), which also deletes the random ID from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing books with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the invite screen.

Children

Sign Book is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.

Contact
