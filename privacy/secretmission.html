Privacy Policy — משימה סודית (Secret Mission)

Last updated: 2026-11-21 (version 1.0.0)

משימה סודית (Secret Mission) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, and you decide who gets a game code. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. The app has no sign-up, no advertising, no purchases, no server for games and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your settings (language, sound), the names typed for the last game, the chosen decks and game length, the game in progress (targets, missions, catches and their times) and a few lifetime counts (games, catches, wins) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. The app never uploads it.

Game codes and reports

When everyone plays on their own phone, the host's phone makes a game code. It contains only the players' names as typed by the host, the chosen decks, the game length, the start time and a random number. The app never sends it anywhere by itself: you press Share and choose where it goes (for example your group chat). At the end, each player can share a report code with their own catches; it contains player numbers and times, no names. Hand-over codes and PINs are shown on screen only. None of this goes to us.

The app never reads your contacts and never asks for phone numbers. The Paste buttons read the clipboard only when you tap them.

Internet use

The game works offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model, your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: no names, no missions, no codes.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

Only internet access (for the update check and the statistics). No camera, microphone, contacts, location, Bluetooth or storage permissions.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. The family deck is written to be safe for children aged 6 and over playing with their families. The app has no ads, no chat, no accounts, and it collects no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
