Privacy Policy — Sky Atlas (אטלס העננים)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Sky Atlas is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your sightings, places, notes, photos and locations stay on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Sky Atlas has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Your sightings

Each sighting you log (what you saw in the sky, the date and time, the place name you type, your notes and an optional photo) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Photos

You can add a photo to a sighting from your camera or your gallery. The app uses the phone's own camera and photo picker, saves a resized copy in its private storage, and shows it only inside the app. Photos are never sent anywhere. Deleting a sighting deletes its photo copy; uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

Your location (optional)

Only if you tap Add my approximate location on a sighting, or turn on Add location to new sightings in Settings, does the app ask for permission to read your approximate location (coarse location, while the app is in use). It reads it once, rounds it to about 1 km (0.01°) and saves it with that sighting on your device. It is never sent anywhere: not to us, not to anyone. The app does not track your location in the background. You can remove the location from any sighting, and you can revoke the permission in your phone's settings.

Where your data is stored

Your sightings, settings (language, theme, haptics, the location option) and photos are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your sightings and settings as text to your clipboard, so you can keep them somewhere you choose. Photos are not included. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sharing and export

When you tap "Share" or "Export to CSV", the app opens your phone's share sheet with a text or a CSV file you can see before sending (for example an atlas entry, a sighting, your list totals and a link to the app's page, or a table of all your sightings including places, rounded locations and notes). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

The sky log, the atlas, the altitude chart, the collection and the identification helper all work entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Sky Atlas sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never your sightings, places, notes, photos or location.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics described above.
  • Approximate location (optional): only when you ask for it, as described above; used on the device only.
  • Camera and photos: the app opens the system camera or photo picker only when you tap Camera or Gallery. On Android it needs no camera or storage permission for this.

Children

Sky Atlas is a general-audience app for ages 13 and up. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
