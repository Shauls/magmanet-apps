Privacy Policy — Letter Duel (דו־קרב אותיות)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Letter Duel (דו־קרב אותיות) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: an online duel (its settings and the moves everyone plays) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the friends you invite see the same board, and only they can see it; you only need a nickname; pass-and-play, practice games, your history and stats stay on your phone; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your online duels, because playing with friends is what the app does: the duel name, the word language and game length, each game's random seed and starter word, and the moves (which letters went on which squares, passes and skipped turns).
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your duels.
  • Reports of offensive moves (which move was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that move.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a duel

Only its members. A duel starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the duel name, the other members' nicknames, the board, the moves and the scores. Nobody else can read a duel: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member.

Games on this phone

Pass-and-play games, practice games against the computer, your game history and your stats are kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to the server.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your duels (so they show offline), moves waiting to be sent, local games, history, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your online duels and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

While a duel is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of duels is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your move waits on the phone and is sent when you are back online.

Reminders

The optional "your move" reminder (off by default) is a local notification scheduled on your phone when a duel is waiting for your move. Nothing about it is sent anywhere, and the server never sends push notifications.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a duel (Players → Leave duel): you are removed from it; your future turns pass by themselves. When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every duel, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your local games, history and nickname are removed from the phone.
  • Duels nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your duels on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the duel's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your online duels. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the duel name, your nickname, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. Nudges and result cards are made on your phone. They only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Letter Duel sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no words, moves or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your online duels with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (asked only when you turn on the reminder) and run at startup: showing and re-scheduling the local reminder.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy code".

Word lists

The word lists come from the word strings of the fastText Common Crawl vectors (Grave et al., 2018, CC BY-SA 3.0). They are part of the app and need no internet.

Children

Letter Duel is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.

Contact
