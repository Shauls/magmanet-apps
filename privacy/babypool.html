Privacy Policy — Baby Pool (ניחושי לידה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Baby Pool (ניחושי לידה) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your pools are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your pools, because playing together is what the app does: the parents' names (as written by the parents), the due date and closing day, the babies' real details entered by the parents after the birth (date, time, weight, length, sex, hair, eye colour, name), each member's guesses, and the wishes members write.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your pools.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data. There is no money in the app: no bets, payments or prizes.

Who can see your pools

Only the members of each pool. A pool starts with the parent who creates it; others join only with the pool's invite code, which members choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the pool, the other members' nicknames, the wishes and, after the closing day, everyone's guesses (before it, the server sends each member only their own guess, the parents included, and the app shows only who has guessed). Nobody else can read a pool: there are no public pools, no search and no profiles. The pool's owner can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. A wish can be reported by members; after three reports it is hidden.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your pools (so they open offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your pools and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; guesses and wishes show by nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a pool is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your pools are refreshed when you open the app or come back to it (and when you pull down to refresh). Without a connection, your guesses and wishes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave the pool (pool menu): you are removed from it and your guesses are deleted; the wishes you wrote stay unless you delete them first.
  • Delete a wish: the writer (or the pool's owner) can delete it.
  • Delete the pool (owner): the pool, all guesses and wishes are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every pool and your anonymous account is deleted from the server.
  • Pools nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted items are kept for up to 30 days so syncing works, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your pools on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave your pools or use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the pool's names and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your pools. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the parents' names, the closing day, the invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The birth card and results you share are made on your phone. Both only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Baby Pool sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no names, guesses, baby details, wishes or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your pools with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen.

Children

Baby Pool is a general-audience app for adults (parents-to-be, family and friends) and is not directed at children.
