Privacy Policy — Memory & Speed Reading (זיכרון וקריאה מהירה)

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Memory & Speed Reading is made by Magmanet. It offers speed-reading and memory exercises. The short version: your texts and results stay on your phone.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive the text you paste, your reading speeds, test answers, scores, the names you give your memory-palace route or any other result. The app has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising. We never sell or share your data.

Data on your phone

  • Results (reading speeds, test scores, drill scores, n-back levels, workout checkmarks and streaks) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete them in Settings ("Reset progress"); uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Pasted text for the readers is kept on your phone only, so you can read it again. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings (language, reading speed, levels, your route names) are saved on your phone.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your settings and progress to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. Where you paste it is up to you.
  • Faces in the names exercise are drawn by the app; it uses no photos and no camera.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short message and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

The exercises need no internet. The app connects to the internet only for: (1) the update check, and (2) the anonymous usage statistics.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "finished a reading test" with its score, or "finished an n-back round" with its level) and for how long. It never includes text you pasted, your answers, the names of your route or anything you typed. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions or requests: through the contact link on https://apps.magmanet.app/
