Privacy Policy — Trip Journal together (יומן טיול משותף)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Trip Journal together (יומן טיול משותף) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a trip journal (its entries, captions and votes) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can write in it too, and only they can see it; your photos never leave your phone; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your trips, because writing together is what the app does: the trip's name, emoji, first day and number of days; each entry (its text, the place name you type, the mood you pick, the daily prompt it answers, the photo caption you type and whether the writer added a photo, plus the day and time of writing); each member's highlight vote and reactions.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your trips.
  • Reports of offensive entries (which entry was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that entry.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

Photos are never uploaded. A photo you add to an entry is copied into the app's private folder on your phone and stays there. The other members only see the caption you write and a note that the photo is on the writer's phone. A photo is included only in a trip book PDF made on your own phone, and only goes where you send that file.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. Place names are whatever you type; the app does not read your location and uses no maps. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a trip

Only its members. A trip starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the trip's details, every entry with its writer's nickname, place, mood and caption, the highlight votes and reactions, and the member list. Nobody else can read a trip: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The trip's creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. Any member can report an offensive entry; after a few reports it is hidden.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your trips (so they show offline), entries waiting to be sent, the photos you added, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your trips and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a trip is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of trips is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your entries and votes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete an entry (entry menu → Delete): it is deleted from the server for everyone, and its photo is deleted from your phone.
  • Leave a trip (trip menu → Leave trip): you are removed from it, your votes and reactions are deleted and its photos are deleted from your phone. The entries you wrote stay in the journal for the others (delete them first if you want them gone). When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a trip (creator): the trip, all its entries, votes and reactions are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every trip, your anonymous account is deleted from the server (DELETE /v1/rooms/me), and your nickname and photos are removed from the phone.
  • Trips nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the photos and the local copy, but not your trips on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the trip's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your trips. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the trip's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The highlights card and the trip book PDF are made on your phone. They only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Trip Journal sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no trips, entries, captions, photos or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your trips with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Camera and photos (only when you tap Camera or Gallery in an entry): the system camera or photo picker opens and the one photo you choose is copied into the app's private folder. On iPhone the app asks for camera / photo library access at that moment.

The app has no access to your contacts, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy".

Children

Trip Journal together is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes
