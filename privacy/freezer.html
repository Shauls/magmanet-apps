Privacy Policy — Freezer Keeper (מה במקפיא)

Last updated: 2026-10-31 · App: com.magmanet.freezer · Developer: Magmanet

English

Freezer Keeper keeps track of the food in your freezer, fridge and pantry, and keeps a shopping list. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Food items you enter (names, food type, place, amount, dates and notes), your storage places, your edits to the storage-time table and your shopping list are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Notifications. If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled locally on your phone. The notification permission is asked only when you turn them on.

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no server for your data.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used and simple counters, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include food names, amounts or dates. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share the list", the shopping list text goes only to the app and person you choose.
  • Backup. "Copy backup" in Settings copies your data as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

Storage times are general guidance

Suggested freezer and fridge times are general guidance from public food-safety charts. Always check the package and the food itself.

Deleting your data

Delete items one by one, or everything in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues

  • פריטי מזון שאתם מזינים (שם, סוג, מקום, כמות, תאריכים והערות), מקומות האחסון, השינויים בטבלת זמני האחסון ורשימת הקניות נשמרים רק באחסון הפרטי של האפליקציה בטלפון.
  • תזכורות מתוזמנות בטלפון עצמו, וההרשאה להתראות מתבקשת רק כשמפעילים אותן.
  • מה יוצא מהטלפון: סטטיסטיקת שימוש אנונימית (בלי שמות מזון, כמויות או תאריכים; אפשר לכבות בהגדרות); בדיקת עדכון יומית; שיתוף רשימת הקניות – רק לאפליקציה ולאדם שבחרתם; גיבוי – מועתק ללוח ואתם מחליטים איפה לשמור.
