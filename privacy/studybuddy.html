Privacy Policy — Study Buddy (לומדים ביחד)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Study Buddy (לומדים ביחד) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a study group (its name, goals and everyone's study minutes) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see it, and only they can see it; your subjects and personal charts stay on your phone; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your study groups, because studying together is what the app does: the group name, what it studies for (bagrut, university exam, psychometric or other), the exam date if you set one, the shared goals (a short text and a number of hours), and for each day each member's study minutes, number of sessions and, while a focus session runs, the time it ends and the subject type (for example "Math" from a fixed list; never a subject name you typed).
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your groups.
  • Reports of an offensive group name or goal (which item was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your school, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

What stays on your phone

Your subjects (their names and colors), the log of your focus sessions and the personal charts made from it, the running timer, your settings and your nickname. They are not sent to the server and are removed when you uninstall the app or tap "Delete all my data".

Who can see a group

Only its members. A group starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the group name, the goals, the exam date, the other members' nicknames, everyone's study minutes, streaks and who is studying right now (until when, and the subject type). Nobody else can read a group: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they show offline), changes waiting to be sent, and the personal data above, in the app's private storage.
  • On the Magmanet server: your groups and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

Notifications

The app's only notification is a local one when a focus session or a break ends. It is scheduled on your phone when you start the timer and is not sent through any server. During a focus session the app sends nothing. You can turn it off in Settings ("Notification when a session ends") or in the phone's settings.

How the app talks to the server

While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds (for "studying now"), and your list of groups is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. When you start or finish a focus session, the app sends your updated minutes for that day to each of your groups. Without a connection, your minutes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a group (group menu → Leave the group): you are removed from it and your minutes in it are deleted. When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a group (creator): the group, its goals and all minutes are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname, subjects and personal stats are removed from the phone.
  • Groups nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the group name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite and "come study with us" messages you share contain the group name, its invite code, how many members are studying at that moment and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. They only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Study Buddy sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no group names, goals, minutes, subjects or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (asked the first time you start a focus session) and run at startup: the local "session finished" notification, kept after the phone restarts.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy".

Children

Study Buddy is meant for teenagers from 13 and adults (high school, university and psychometric students). It is not directed at younger children.

Changes
