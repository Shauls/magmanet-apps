Privacy Policy — Book Club (מועדון קריאה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Book Club (מועדון קריאה) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your clubs are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your clubs, because running a book club together is what the app does: the club name and emblem, the books members suggest (title, author, page count and a short note), votes, the reading schedule, each member's reading progress, comments, discussion questions, meetings (date, time and place) and RSVPs, ratings and short reviews.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your clubs.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts, your photos or your location. Book covers are drawn by the app from the title; nothing is looked up online. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see your clubs

Only the members of each club. A club starts with its host; others join only with the club's invite link or code, which someone in the club chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the club's books, votes, progress, comments, questions, meetings, ratings and the other members' nicknames. Nobody else can read a club: there are no public clubs, no search and no profiles. The host can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. Everything written by someone else has a "Report" action; after several reports it is hidden for everyone.

Spoiler protection (comments on a part of the book stay hidden until you mark that part as read) is a reading aid done by the app on your phone. Comments are shared with all members of the club, as they are written for the club.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your clubs (so they work offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your clubs and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a club is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of clubs is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a club (club menu → Leave the club): you are removed from it and your votes, reading progress and RSVPs are deleted; what you wrote (suggestions, comments, questions, reviews) stays for the club unless you delete it first. If you were the host, the longest-standing member takes over; when the last member leaves, the club is deleted.
  • Delete anything you wrote from its menu.
  • Delete a club (host): the club and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every club and your anonymous account is deleted from the server.
  • Clubs nobody has changed for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted items are kept for up to 30 days so that syncing works, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your clubs on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave your clubs or use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the club name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your clubs. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message, the "vote for our next book" message, the meeting reminder and the "year in books" card you share contain the club name, the invite code where relevant, book titles, the meeting time and place, and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. They are created on your phone and only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Book Club sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a book was suggested or rated). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no club names, book titles, comments or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your clubs with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen.

Children

Book Club is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
