Privacy Policy — Crossroads (פרשת דרכים)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Crossroads is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your decisions, options, scores, notes and journal stay on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Crossroads has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Your decisions

Everything you enter (decision titles, options, criteria and weights, scores, pros and cons, 10/10/10 feelings and notes, coin results, the option you chose and why, and how it turned out) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Scoring with friends

To ask friends for their scores, the app creates a short code containing the decision's title, options, criteria and weights, and the name you choose to show (optional). It never contains your own scores, pros and cons or notes. The code goes out only if you send it, through your phone's share sheet or by copying it. A friend who pastes it into their Crossroads app scores the options and sends back a code with their name and scores; you paste it into your app. The codes travel only in your own messages. There is no server, and Magmanet never sees them.

Where your data is stored

Your decisions and settings (language, theme, haptics, your name on invites) are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your decisions and settings as text to your clipboard, so you can keep them somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sharing

When you tap "Share as image" or "Share as text", the app shows or prepares a summary of the decision (the options, their totals, the reason the winner won and a link to the app's page) and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

Every method, chart and the journal work entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Crossroads sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example "a template was used" or "the coin was flipped"). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never your decision titles, options, criteria, scores, notes, names or what you chose.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics described above.

The app asks for no other permission: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

Children

Crossroads is a general-audience app for ages 13 and up. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
