Privacy Policy — Pickup Game (משחק שכונה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Pickup Game (משחק שכונה) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your groups are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your groups, because organizing the game together is what the app does: the group name, sport, usual place and map link, weekly day and time; the games (time, place, spots, note, cancelled or not); each member's answer (In, Maybe or Out, and when), MVP vote and "I'll bring the ball"; guest first names the organizer adds; teams, bib colors and the score (who scored).
  • Skill levels for fair teams: in each group, the levels you give (the organizer's, or each member's ratings of the others). The server sends a level only to the organizer (whose phone balances the teams) and to the person who gave it; other members never receive it, and the app never displays anyone's level.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your groups.
  • Reports: if you report a game, the server counts the report.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see your groups

Only the members of each group. A group starts with its organizer; others join only with the group's invite link or code, which a member chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the games, the answers, guest names, teams, scores, MVP votes and the other members' nicknames (not the skill levels: those go only to the organizer). Nobody else can read a group: there are no public groups, no search and no profiles. The organizer can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they work offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your settings and this copy to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your groups and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; members are shown by nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your groups are refreshed when you open the app or come back to it (and when you pull down to refresh). The "spot opened" notice appears inside the app when it sees you moved up from the waitlist. Without a connection, your changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a group (group menu → Leave group): you are removed from it and your answers, votes, levels and "I'll bring the ball" are removed. If you were the organizer, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a group (organizer): the group and all its games are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every group and your anonymous account is deleted from the server.
  • Groups nobody has used for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted games are kept for up to 30 days so syncing works, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave your groups or use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the group name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Open map opens the game's map link (or a map search for the place) in your maps app or browser; that service then sees the request.

The messages you share (invites, "we need 2 more", teams, results) contain the group name, game details, nicknames, the invite code and links to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. They are created on your phone and only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Pickup Game sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a group was created or an invite was shared). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no group names, games or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen.

Children

Pickup Game is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
