Privacy Policy — Daily Standup (סטנדאפ יומי)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Daily Standup (סטנדאפ יומי) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a team (its questions and everyone's check-ins) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see it, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the reminder stays on your phone; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your teams, because sharing check-ins is what the app does: the team name and icon, its questions, its workdays and whether it asks about mood.
  • Your check-ins: your answers for each day, the mood face you picked (if any), whether your blocker is solved, the kudos you give, and "I can help" when you offer help with someone's blocker.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your teams.
  • Reports of offensive check-ins (which check-in was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. It does not connect to any work tool, chat or calendar. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a team

Only its members. A team starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example in your team chat). Members see the questions, every member's answers, mood faces, kudos and who offered help. Nobody else can read a team: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The team admin can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your teams (so they show offline), check-ins waiting to be sent, your nickname, the reminder time and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your teams, check-ins and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

While a team is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of teams is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your check-ins wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone for your teams' workdays at the time you choose. It is created and shown by your phone only; nothing about it is sent anywhere.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a team (team settings → Leave team): you are removed from it, and your kudos and "I can help" offers are removed. The answers you wrote stay with the team (the admin can delete the team). When the admin leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a team (admin): the team and all its check-ins are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every team, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname and reminder are removed from the phone.
  • Teams nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your teams on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the team name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your teams. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the team name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The weekly summary (text or PDF) is made on your phone. Both only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Daily Standup sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no team names, questions, answers or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your teams with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (asked only when you turn the reminder on), run at startup and vibrate: the local check-in reminder.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy".

Children

Daily Standup is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
