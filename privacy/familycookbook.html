Privacy Policy — Family Cookbook (ספר המתכונים של המשפחה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Family Cookbook (ספר המתכונים של המשפחה) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your family cookbook is stored on the Magmanet server so that the relatives you invite can see it, and only they can; you only need a nickname; photos never leave your phone; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • The cookbook, because collecting recipes together is what the app does: the recipes members type in (title, who it is from, a story, ingredients, steps, chapter, holiday, kosher and diet tags, servings and time), tips, "I made it!" and heart reactions, suggestions sent to a recipe's author, which suggestions the author marked as done, and which member added each entry.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your cookbooks.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a cookbook

Only the members of that cookbook. A cookbook starts with its creator; others join only with its invite code or link, which a member chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the recipes, tips, reactions, suggestions and the other members' nicknames. Nobody else can read a cookbook: there are no public cookbooks, no search and no profiles. The owner can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Photos stay on your phone

Photos of dishes are kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded and other members don't see them. They go into the cookbook PDF only if you keep "Photos of the dishes" on and then share the PDF yourself.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your cookbooks (so they work offline), changes waiting to be sent, photos, the steps you ticked off while cooking, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies the settings and the local copy of the cookbooks to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your cookbooks and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; entries show who added them by nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a cookbook is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of cookbooks is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a cookbook (cookbook menu → Cookbook settings → Leave the cookbook): you are removed from it; your recipes and tips stay for the family, and your reactions and suggestions are removed. Photos of that cookbook are deleted from your phone. If you were the owner, the next member becomes the owner; when the last member leaves, the cookbook is deleted from the server.
  • Delete a recipe or tip: the person who added it, or the cookbook owner, can delete it.
  • Delete the cookbook (owner): the cookbook and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every cookbook, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and photos on your phone are deleted.
  • Cookbooks nobody has changed for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted entries are kept for up to 30 days so that "undo" and syncing work, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your cookbooks on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave them or use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the cookbook's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your cookbooks. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message, a shared recipe, a shared menu and the cookbook PDF are created on your phone and only go where you send them. The invite contains the cookbook's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site; the code sits after the # in the link, so it is not sent to the site's server.

Anonymous usage statistics

Family Cookbook sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a recipe was added or a cookbook was printed). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no recipes, stories, tips or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your cookbooks with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Camera / photos (only when you tap "Take a photo" or "Choose a photo" on a recipe): the chosen photo is copied into the app's private storage on your phone.

The app has no access to your contacts, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen.

Children

Family Cookbook is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
