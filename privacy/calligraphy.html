Privacy Policy — Nib Studio (קולמוס)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Nib Studio is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Nib Studio has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your stars and best scores for each letter, your streak and daily words, the ink on your quote card, and your settings (language, pen, ink, paper, sound, music, theme, haptics) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Reset progress (the card and settings stay until uninstall).

What you write is drawn by the app from your finger or stylus movements; it is not handwriting recognition and nothing you write is sent anywhere.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sharing

When you tap Share on the daily word or Save as picture on a quote card, the app draws a picture of your writing on paper and opens your phone's share sheet with the picture, a short text and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. The picture is made by the app; it never uses your phone's camera or photos.

Internet use

The app works entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Nib Studio sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent, and never anything you write.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics described above.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

Nib Studio is a general-audience app for ages 13 and up. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
