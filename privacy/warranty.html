Privacy Policy — Warranty Vault (כספת אחריות)

Last updated: 2026-10-31 · App: com.magmanet.warranty · Developer: Magmanet

English

Warranty Vault keeps your receipts and warranty cards and reminds you before a warranty ends. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Items you enter (product, store, purchase date, price, warranty length, serial number, notes) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Camera / gallery. When you add a receipt or warranty card photo, you take it with the camera app or pick it with the system photo picker. A copy is saved only in the app's private folder. It is not added to your gallery and never uploaded.
  • Notifications. Reminders before a warranty ends are scheduled locally on your phone.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used and simple counters, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include items, prices, notes or photos. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share", the item's details and its photos go only to the app you choose.
  • Backup file. If you tap "Backup file with photos", a zip file with all your items and photos is handed to the app you choose (for example your own cloud drive or email). Keep it private. It is not sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete an item to remove it and its photos. Restoring a backup replaces the items on the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
