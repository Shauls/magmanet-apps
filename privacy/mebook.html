Privacy Policy — MeBook (מיבוק)

Last updated: 2026-11-02 · App: com.magmanet.mebook · Developer: Magmanet

English

MeBook opens the official websites of social networks you choose, in one place. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these networks.

Websites you open have their own policies

Each network or site you open runs in a web view on your phone, and it works as it would in a browser: it gets your address, what you do on it, and the cookies it sets, under its own privacy policy. We have no control over that and no access to it. Please read each network's policy.

What the app never does

  • It never sees, stores or handles your passwords. You sign in on the network's own page.
  • It never reads the content of the pages or their cookies, adds script or styles to them, or extracts anything from them.
  • It never posts for you. "Post to several" opens each network's page, and you press Post.

What stays on your phone

  • Your list: which networks are shown, their order, and the names and addresses of sites you add.
  • Time counts: the time a network is on screen in this app, per day, kept for 35 days. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Limits and the reminder: daily limits, and the time of the optional reminder.
  • Sign-ins and site data: cookies, cache and site storage belong to the web view on your phone. They are sent only to the site they came from, as in any browser.

What leaves the phone

  • The websites you open, as described above.
  • What you write in "Post to several": nothing until you tap "Open the page". Then the text and link go in the address of that network's own share page, so that network receives them even if you don't press Post. For networks without a share page, the text is copied to your clipboard.
  • Files you upload go only to the website where you chose them. The app doesn't keep them.
  • Links that lead elsewhere open in your browser or the matching app.
  • Anonymous usage statistics go over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. They include the app name and version, your operating-system version (Android or iOS), your language, how the app was installed (on Android: from Google Play, the website or elsewhere; on iPhone and iPad: the App Store or TestFlight), a random install ID made by the app, anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used, for how long, and how many networks a post goes to) and counts of errors by type (a short code, never a message or any content). On Android they also include your phone's manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone); on iPhone and iPad they don't include the device model. They never include web addresses, which networks you use, your time counts, or anything you type. On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored; raw data is deleted after 400 days. They are used only to count active users and improve the app. They are on by default; turn them off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"), which stops sending and deletes the random ID and unsent events from your phone.
  • The update check (Android only): once a day the app asks the Magmanet server whether a newer version exists, sending only the app name, its version and how it was installed; the server stores no identifiers and no IP address. If the server is unavailable the app downloads a public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). You can turn this off in Settings. On iPhone and iPad there is no update check: the App Store handles updates.
  • Backup: "Copy backup" in Settings copies your list, added sites and time counts as text to the clipboard (no sign-ins). Where you paste it is up to you.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the websites you open, the statistics and, on Android, the update check. (iOS has no internet permission to grant.)
  • Notifications: only for the daily reminder, asked only when you turn it on. The reminder is scheduled on your phone and its text is generic.
  • On iPhone and iPad, camera, microphone and photos: only if a website's upload field offers them and you choose that, through the system's own prompt. What you capture goes only to that website.
  • On Android: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permission. Uploads use the system file picker. Sites asking for the camera, microphone or location are refused.

Deleting your data

  • One network: use Sign out in the network's own menu. Inside the app all sites share one sign-in store, so the app can't clear one network alone. You can erase a network's time count in the app.
  • All networks: "Sign out of all networks" in Settings deletes the cookies, cache and site data of every site in the app.
  • Everything: "Clear everything" in Settings also deletes your list, added sites, time counts, limits and the reminder. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Data held by the networks themselves is managed in your account with them.

Children

The app is not directed at children. The networks have their own age rules.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
