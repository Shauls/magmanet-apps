Privacy Policy — Little Science Lab

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Little Science Lab (מעבדת המדע / Petit labo des sciences) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your child's data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Little Science Lab has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics. We never see, sell or share any data.

Where your data is stored

The stickers and stars your child earns, the lab notebook drawings (with the guess made for each experiment) and the settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app, or when a grown-up uses "Reset progress" in Settings.

Reading aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not record, store or send any audio, and it never uses the microphone.

Internet use

Little Science Lab works entirely offline. The only time it goes online is the update check. Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). The check carries nothing about your child or your device. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice, and the Update button asks a grown-up question (a multiplication) before opening the download link. A grown-up can turn the check off in Settings → For grown-ups.

Permissions

The app's only permission is internet access, used just for the daily update check described above. It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

Little Science Lab is designed for children aged 6 to 11. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat or social features and no sharing, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The only link out of the app (the update download) and resetting progress are behind a parental gate. Ideas to try at home are always presented as something to do with a grown-up, together with a safety note.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
