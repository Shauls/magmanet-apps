Privacy Policy — Birthday Circle (ימי הולדת של החבר׳ה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Birthday Circle (ימי הולדת של החבר׳ה) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the birthdays added to a group are stored on the Magmanet server so that the group's members can see them and get reminders, and only they can see them; a year you choose to hide never leaves your phone; "My people" stay on your phone only; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • The birthdays added to your groups, because sharing them with the group is what the app does: for each entry a name (your nickname for your own birthday, or the name you type for someone you add, such as a child), the day and month, and only if you choose: the year and a Hebrew birthday (Hebrew day and month).
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your groups, and the group's name and kind (class, work, friends…).
  • "I sent wishes" marks (a reaction on a birthday) and reports of wrong or offensive entries (which entry, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that entry.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Hiding your year

When you turn on "Hide my year", the year is kept only on your phone (to fill in your next groups) and is never sent to the server. Note that a Hebrew birthday together with the date can hint at the year; the app says so when you choose both.

My people

Birthdays you add under "My people" (people who are not in the app) are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to the server.

Who can see a group

Only its members. A group starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the group's name, the other members' nicknames, every birthday in the group and the "I sent wishes" counts. Nobody else can read a group: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The admin (the group's creator) can remove entries and members and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they show offline), changes waiting to be sent, your own birthday, My people, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your groups, with the birthdays and nicknames in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone (the day before and on the day, at 9:00). No push service is used and nothing is sent to schedule them. You can turn them off in Settings.

How the app talks to the server

While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your groups are refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, what you add waits on the phone and is sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a group (group menu → Leave group): you are removed from it and your "I sent wishes" marks are deleted; the birthdays you added stay in the group (delete them first if you want them gone). When the admin leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete an entry you added (or, as admin, any entry): it is deleted from the server.
  • Delete a group (admin): the group and all its entries are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account is deleted from the server (DELETE /v1/rooms/me), and your birthday, My people and nickname are removed from the phone.
  • Groups nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the group's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the group's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. A greeting you send is text or a picture made on your phone, optionally with a link to the app's page. Both only go where you send them. "Collect for a gift together" opens the Group Gift app's page on the Magmanet apps site in your browser.

Anonymous usage statistics

Birthday Circle sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no names, dates, groups or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (Android 13+, asked once): birthday reminders.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): keeps scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy".

Children

Birthday Circle is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. Parents may add a child's name and birthday to a class group; only that group's members can see it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
