Privacy Policy — Family Tree together (עץ משפחה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Family Tree together (עץ משפחה) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your family tree is stored on the Magmanet server so that the relatives you invite can see it, and only they can; you only need a nickname; photos never leave your phone; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • The family tree, because building it together is what the app does: for each person the details that members type in (names in Hebrew and English, gender, birth year or date, place of birth, whether and when they passed away, a short story), the links between people (parents, children, spouses), which member added each entry, which member said "this is me", and suggested edits waiting for approval.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your trees.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Information about other people. A family tree contains details about relatives, some of them living. Please add only what your family is comfortable sharing within the family, and respect a relative's wish to be left out or to have their details removed. The person who added an entry and the tree owner can change or delete it; any member can report an entry.

Who can see a tree

Only the members of that tree. A tree starts with its creator; others join only with the tree's invite code, which a member chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the people in the tree, their details and the other members' nicknames. Nobody else can read a tree: there are no public trees, no search and no profiles. The owner can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Photos stay on your phone

Photos you add to a person's card are kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded and other members don't see them. They are included in a poster only if you turn on "Include photos" and then share the poster yourself.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your trees (so they work offline), changes waiting to be sent, photos, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies the settings and the local copy of the trees to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your trees and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; entries show who added them by nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a tree is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of trees is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a tree (tree menu → Tree settings → Leave the tree): you are removed from it; the people you added stay in the tree for the family. Photos of that tree are deleted from your phone. If you were the owner, the next member becomes the owner; when the last member leaves, the tree is deleted from the server.
  • Delete a person: the person who added them, or the tree owner, can delete them and their links.
  • Delete the tree (owner): the tree and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every tree, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and photos on your phone are deleted.
  • Trees nobody has changed for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted entries are kept for up to 30 days so that "undo" and syncing work, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your trees on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave your trees or use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the tree's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your trees. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the tree's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. It is created on your phone and only goes where you send it.

Anonymous usage statistics

Family Tree together sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a tree was created or joined). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no names, dates, stories or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your trees with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Camera / photos (only when you tap "Take a photo" or "Choose from gallery" on a person's card): the chosen photo is copied into the app's private storage on your phone.

The app has no access to your contacts, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen.

Children

Family Tree together is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
