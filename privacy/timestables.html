Privacy Policy — Times Table Race

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Times Table Race (מרוץ לוח הכפל / La course des tables) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Times Table Race has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Each child's first name (or nickname), chosen racer, stars, trophies, race results and which multiplication facts they answered right or wrong are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app, and a grown-up can clear a child's progress or remove a child in the grown-ups area.

The app's Copy backup feature (grown-ups area → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

Times Table Race works offline. The only time it goes online is the update check. Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). The check carries no identifiers and nothing about your child or your device. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and a grown-up (behind the grown-up check) chooses whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in the grown-ups area ("Check for updates automatically").

The Android "internet" permission is also needed for the direct local connection between two phones in the same room (see below); that connection never goes to the internet.

Racing on two phones: what is exchanged

When two (or more) children race on phones next to each other, these go directly between those phones over the local Wi-Fi (or Bluetooth, if a grown-up turned it on), and nowhere else: the child's racer name and racer emoji, the host's race settings (which tables, how many questions), and each racer's progress (questions solved and mistakes). Nothing goes to the internet or to us, and nothing is kept on the other phone after the race.

Permissions

  • Internet / Wi-Fi / local network: the daily update check, and finding the other phone on the same Wi-Fi when the children choose "Race on two phones".
  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and location (optional): only if a grown-up turns on "Also use Bluetooth" in the two-phone lobby (it is off by default and behind the grown-up check). The app then uses Google Nearby Connections to link the phones directly. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth discovery on some versions, so the app asks for it then. The app never reads, stores or sends a location.

No camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

Times Table Race is designed for children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app (except the update download link, which is behind a grown-up check) and no chat or social features, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Names typed into the app stay on the device (and, during a two-phone race, are shown on the other phone in the room). The grown-ups area, the update link and the Bluetooth option are behind a grown-up check (a multiplication beyond the 10 × 10 table).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
