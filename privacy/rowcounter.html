Privacy Policy — Stitch Tally (שורות ועיניים)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Stitch Tally is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your projects, notes, photos and PDFs stay on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Stitch Tally has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your projects (names, counters, reminders, notes, yarn and needle details, time worked), your yarn stash and your settings (language, sounds, keep-screen-on, volume-button counting, the timer option, theme, haptics) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you delete them in the app or when you uninstall the app.

Photos and PDFs

When you add a photo, you choose to take one with the camera or pick one from your gallery; when you add a pattern PDF, you pick it from your phone's files. The app keeps a private copy in its own folder on your device and shows it only to you. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the photo, the PDF or the project deletes the copy.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your projects, stash and settings as text to your clipboard (without photos and PDFs), so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sharing progress

When you tap "Share progress", the app opens your phone's share sheet with the project name, its count and time worked, and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

Counting and everything else works entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Stitch Tally sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never project names, notes, photos, PDFs or counts.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Camera (only when you choose "Take a photo"): the photo is taken by your phone's camera app and handed to Stitch Tally. On iPhone the system asks first.
  • Photos (only when you choose "From the gallery"): you pick one photo in the system picker; the app sees only that photo.

The volume buttons are read only while the counting screen is open and only if you turn on "Count with the volume buttons". The app requests no microphone, contacts or location permissions.

Children

Stitch Tally is a general-audience utility. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
