Privacy Policy — Pot Clocks (שעון לכל סיר)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Pot Clocks is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your timers and presets stay on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Pot Clocks has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your timers (names, steps, colours, tones and their end times), your saved presets and your settings (language, sounds, spoken announcements, keep-screen-on, how long alarms ring, theme, haptics) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you delete them in the app or when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your timers, presets and settings as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Notifications and alarms

When a timer is running, the app asks your phone to show a notification when it ends, so you hear it even when the app is closed. These notifications are scheduled and shown by your phone itself; nothing is sent to a server.

Spoken announcements

If you turn on "Say it out loud", the app uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice to say things like "Rice is ready". The text is spoken on the device; nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere by the app.

Sharing the app

When you tap "Share the app", the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short message and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

Timers, alarms and everything else work entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Pot Clocks sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never timer names, presets or times.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (asked the first time you start a timer): to alert you when a timer ends while the app is in the background or closed.
  • Alarms and reminders / exact alarms (Android): so those notifications arrive at the exact second. On newer Android versions you allow it from Settings in the app.
  • Run at startup (Android): to keep scheduled timer alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: to vibrate when a timer starts and ends (you can turn this off).

The app keeps the screen on while timers are set (a setting) using a window flag, not a permission. It requests no microphone, camera, contacts or location permissions.

Children

Pot Clocks is a general-audience utility. It collects no personal information from anyone.
