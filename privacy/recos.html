Privacy Policy — Recos (המלצות מחברים)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Recos (המלצות מחברים) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a group's recommendations, questions and reactions are stored on the Magmanet server so that the friends you invite can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your groups, because a shared board is what the app does: the group's name and icon, the recommendations members write (what, category, why, stars, who it's for and an optional link), questions to the group, and reactions ("added to my list", "tried it", agree, disagree, and your stars after trying).
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your groups.
  • Reports of an inappropriate recommendation or question (which item, by which anonymous account), used only to hide it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your real name, your contacts or your location, and it never uploads photos. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a group

Only its members. A group starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the group's recommendations and questions, the other members' nicknames, and who reacted to what (for example who added a tip to their list). Your personal list is built from your own "added to my list" reactions, so the friend who recommended something sees that you saved it. Nobody else can read a group: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The organizer can delete posts, remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they show offline), actions waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your groups and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of groups is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your actions wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

Links in recommendations

A recommendation may include a link a member typed. The app never opens or fetches it by itself; when you tap it, the app asks first and then opens it in your browser. That website is a third party with its own privacy policy.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete a recommendation or question you wrote (its menu → Delete). The organizer can delete any post.
  • Leave a group (group menu → Leave group): you are removed from it and your reactions and stars are deleted; your recommendations stay on the board for the others. When the organizer leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a group (organizer): the group and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname is removed from the phone.
  • Groups nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the group's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite, question, recommendation and digest messages you share contain the group's name, the recommendations and nicknames they mention, the invite code or link and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The digest card you share is a picture made on your phone. They only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Recos sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no recommendations, questions, group names or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy the invite".

Children

Recos is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
