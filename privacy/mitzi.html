Privacy Policy — Mitzi's World

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Mitzi's World is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Mitzi's World has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

Mitzi's World works entirely offline and does not connect to the internet.

Permissions

Mitzi's World requests no device permissions: no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

  • Text-to-speech (a feature, not a permission): the app reads words and instructions aloud using your device's built-in speech engine. Nothing is recorded.

Children

Mitzi's World is designed for children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app and no chat or social features, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Settings and progress reports are behind a parent gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
