Privacy Policy — ARMA

Last updated: 2026-10-15

ARMA is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: we (Magmanet) don't receive any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your projects stay on your device. When you look up a site, the address or block/parcel you typed and the site's coordinates are sent directly from your device to the public open-data services listed below, only to fetch the results you asked for.

ARMA is not a government app and does not represent the Israel Planning Administration or any government body. It reads public open data published by the services listed below.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

When you search for a site or open its map, the app contacts these public services directly from your device:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org): the address you typed, to find its location.
  • Israel Planning Administration open map services (https://ags.iplan.gov.il/arcgisiplan/rest/services/PlanningPublic): the site's coordinates, to find the plans, land-use zones and conservation designations there.
  • GovMap open data (https://open.govmap.gov.il/geoserver/opendata/wfs): the site's coordinates or the block/parcel numbers, to find the parcel and its registered area.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles (https://tile.openstreetmap.org): the map squares around the site, to show the mini-map. You can turn the map off in Settings.

These are searched sites, not your own location: the app has no location permission and never reads where your device is. Like any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address and handles requests under its own policy (OpenStreetMap Foundation: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy; Israeli government services: https://www.gov.il/he/departments/general/privacy_policy). Magmanet does not receive, see or store these requests.

If you choose to connect your own ARMA server in Settings, site lookups and your projects are sent to that server instead, under your organisation's terms.

What we collect

Nothing. Magmanet has no server for this app and receives no personal data, usage data or crash reports. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app. You can clear your search history in Settings.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Permissions

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): to contact the public services above, the optional ARMA server you configure, and the map tiles. The app requests no other permissions.
  • Opening links: if you choose to open a link (for example a plan page on mavat.iplan.gov.il), it opens in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

Your projects

The sites, assumptions, notes and reports you create are stored only on your device unless you connect your own ARMA server in Settings; then they are synchronized with that server under your organisation's terms. An API key you enter is kept on the device (and in backups you copy). Sample sites use fictional numbers. ARMA's estimates are for early screening only and are not professional planning, appraisal or legal advice.

Children

ARMA is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
