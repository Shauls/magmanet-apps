Privacy Policy — Trail Tracker (שביל)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Trail Tracker is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your locations, tracks and photos stay on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which never include a location and which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Trail Tracker has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Your location and tracks

The app reads your device's precise location only while you record a hike that you started by tapping "Start a hike". While it records, an ongoing notification ("Recording your hike") is shown on Android, and the system location indicator on iPhone. Recording stops when you tap Finish. The app does not request background-location permission and never reads your location at any other time.

The recorded track (positions, times, altitudes and their accuracy), the hike name and its statistics are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere: not to us, not to anyone. There is no map server: the track is drawn by the app itself, without downloading map tiles.

Barometer

On phones that have one, the app reads the air-pressure sensor while recording to measure climb and descent more accurately. The readings are used on the device only and are not stored separately.

Photos

You can take a photo during or after a hike. The app uses the phone's own camera, saves a copy in its private storage, and pins it to a point on your track. Photos are never sent anywhere. Deleting a photo or a hike deletes the copy; uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

Safety plan and reminder

"Share my plan" creates a text with the route name you type, your planned return time and an optional note, and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. If you turn on the reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your phone for your return time. The plan is stored only on your device and is cleared when you tap "I'm back".

Where your data is stored

Your hikes, photos, plan and settings (language, units, theme, haptics, battery saver, barometer) are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Sharing, export and import

When you tap Share, the app creates on your phone a text summary, a picture of your track, or a GPX file (which contains the positions and times of the track) and opens the share sheet. "Export all hikes" does the same with one GPX file per hike. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. Temporary copies made for sharing are deleted the next time the app starts. Importing a GPX file reads the file you pick, on the device only.

Internet use

Recording, the track drawings, the history and all statistics work entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings.

Anonymous usage statistics

Trail Tracker sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used, such as "hike started" or "GPX exported", and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never your location, tracks, hike names, notes, plan or photos.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Precise location (while using the app): to record the hike you start; used on the device only.
  • Foreground service (location) and wake lock: keep the recording going with the screen off, with a visible notification.
  • Notifications: the recording notification and the optional safety reminder.
  • Run at startup: only to restore a scheduled safety reminder after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics described above.
  • Camera: the app opens the system camera only when you tap Photo.

Children

Trail Tracker is a general-audience app for ages 13 and up. It collects no personal information from anyone.
