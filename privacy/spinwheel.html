Privacy Policy — Spin & Decide (גלגל החלטות)

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · App: com.magmanet.spinwheel · Developer: Magmanet

English

Spin & Decide is a decision wheel, team splitter and random picker. Everything you type stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Wheels you make or edit (titles, options, weights, colours) are stored on your device.
  • Groups (group names, the first names or nicknames you type, skill stars, keep-apart / keep-together pairs, and who already had a turn in the picker) are stored on your device.
  • History of the last 200 spins, team splits, picks, rolls and flips is stored on your device. You can clear it.
  • Settings (language, sounds, options) are stored on your device.

None of this is sent to us. The app has no account and no server for your data. On Android it is also excluded from Google's automatic app backup. On iPhone, a backup of the whole device that you make to iCloud or a computer may include the app's data, like that of other apps.

Randomness

Results are drawn on the device with the operating system's secure random generator. Nothing about a spin is sent anywhere.

What leaves the device

  • What you share. A result, a pick or the teams (as text or a picture) go only to the app, person or place you choose in the share sheet.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (features used, simple counters and counts of error types, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include names, wheel options or results. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check (Android) asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Copy backup in Settings copies your wheels, groups and settings as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

Permissions

Internet only (for the two items above). No camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permission.

Deleting your data

Delete wheels and groups in the app and clear the history from the History screen. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Things you already shared are not affected.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues

Français

Roue des décisions est une roue des décisions, un outil pour faire des équipes et un tirage au sort. Tout ce que vous saisissez reste sur votre appareil : roues, groupes (prénoms, étoiles, paires), historique et réglages. Rien n'est envoyé au développeur ; les données sont exclues de la sauvegarde automatique de Google sur Android. Les résultats sont tirés sur l'appareil par le générateur aléatoire sécurisé du système.

Ce qui sort de l'appareil : ce que vous partagez, vers la destination choisie ; des statistiques d'utilisation anonymes (sans prénoms, options ni résultats ; désactivables dans les réglages) ; la vérification quotidienne des mises à jour (Android) ; « Copier une sauvegarde » copie vos données dans le presse-papiers. Seule l'autorisation Internet est demandée. Désinstaller l'application supprime tout.
