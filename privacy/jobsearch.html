Privacy Policy — Job Search (חיפוש עבודה)

Last updated: 2026-11-04 · App: com.magmanet.jobsearch · Developer: Magmanet

English

Job Search keeps track of the jobs you apply to and builds your CV. Everything it saves stays on your phone. There is no account.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Jobs you enter (company, role, link, source, salary range, contact person, notes, stage, interviews and checklists) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your CV details (name, phone, email, city, web addresses, experience, education, service, skills, languages) are stored the same way. The PDF is created on the phone.
  • Notifications. Follow-up and interview reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. You can keep company names off the lock screen with "Discreet notifications" in Settings.
  • The system's cloud backup is turned off for this app, so this data isn't copied there.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used and simple counters, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include anything you typed. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Links. If you open a job link, your browser opens it, and that site's own policy applies.
  • Your CV. If you tap "Share PDF", the file goes only to the app you choose.
  • Backup file. If you tap "Backup file", a file with all your jobs and CV details is handed to the app you choose (for example your own cloud drive or email). Keep it private. It is not sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete a job, an interview or a CV entry in the app. Restoring a backup replaces what is on the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues

  • המשרות (חברה, תפקיד, קישור, מקור, טווח שכר, איש קשר, הערות, שלב, ראיונות ורשימות הכנה) נשמרות רק באחסון הפרטי של האפליקציה ולא נשלחות אלינו או לאף אחד.
  • פרטי קורות החיים (שם, טלפון, מייל, עיר, כתובות אינטרנט, ניסיון, השכלה, שירות, כישורים, שפות) נשמרים באותו אופן. קובץ ה-PDF נוצר בטלפון.

  • מחיקה: אפשר למחוק משרה, ראיון או רשומה באפליקציה. הסרת האפליקציה מוחקת הכול.
