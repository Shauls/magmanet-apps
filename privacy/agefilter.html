Privacy Policy — איך אראה? (Age Filter)

Last updated: 2026-10-26

איך אראה? (Age Filter) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: faces are found on your phone, and your camera picture and photos never leave it unless you share a picture yourself. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. The app has no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your face, your camera picture or your photos. We never sell or share your data.

The app uses Google's ML Kit library for face detection. ML Kit may send anonymous diagnostic and performance information to Google, such as the device model and Android version, the app's name and version, how long detection took, error codes, and a per-installation identifier that doesn't identify you or your device. It never includes your photos, your camera picture, your face or face data. Google encrypts it in transit and doesn't pass it to third parties. Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

The camera and face detection

  • The app uses the front camera only while the camera screen is open. The camera permission is asked the first time you tap "Start camera", after a short explanation in the app.
  • Each camera frame is analysed on your phone by Google ML Kit face detection, which runs on the device without an internet connection. It finds where the eyes, nose and mouth are, and how the head is tilted. The app uses these points only to draw the cartoon stickers and the photo warp in the right place, and then drops the frame. Face data is processed on the device only: it is never stored, never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and it is not used to recognise or identify anyone. (The face detection model is part of the app; the only thing ML Kit sends to Google is the anonymous diagnostic information described above.)
  • The looks ("old me", "baby me", costumes and so on) are cartoon drawings and a gentle stretch of the photo, just for fun. They are not a prediction of how you will look and nothing about them is stored.

Photos

  • "Use a photo from the gallery" opens the system photo picker; the app sees only the photo you pick. Faces in it are found on the phone in the same way. The photo is not copied, kept or uploaded by the app.
  • A picture is saved only when you tap Save (to your phone's gallery, in an "Age Filter" album). On Android 9 and older the phone asks for storage permission at that moment; on iPhone the system asks whether the app may add photos to your library (add-only access).
  • Share makes a temporary copy of the finished picture in the app's cache and opens the system share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app never uploads it itself. The share text includes a link to the app's page. Once shared, the recipient's app handles the picture under its own privacy policy.

Where your other data is stored

Your settings (language, last look, strength, whether the app label is on, whether you've seen the camera explanation) are saved only on your device. The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies these settings as text to your clipboard — never pictures — so you can keep them somewhere you choose. The app never uploads it.

Internet use

The filters work offline, and the app never uploads your pictures. The internet is used for the update check, the anonymous usage statistics below, and ML Kit's diagnostic information (see above).

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens are opened, which buttons such as Save, Share or Before / after are used, and for how long the app is used). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No pictures, no camera frames, no face data and no file names are ever sent.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: asked only the first time you tap "Start camera", after a short explanation in the app. If you say no, the app explains how to allow it later in the phone's settings; gallery photos keep working.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) / Add to Photos (iPhone): asked only when you tap Save.
  • Internet: for the update check, the anonymous usage statistics and ML Kit's diagnostics only.

No microphone, location or contacts permissions. Gallery photos use the system photo picker.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. Its content is clean and kids may enjoy it with their families, but it is not directed at children under 13. It has no ads, no chat and no accounts, and it collects no personal information.
