Privacy Policy — Semantika

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Semantika is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, unless you choose to join the online leaderboards. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Semantika has no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

The other exception is the optional online leaderboards (below): if, and only if, you join them, the nickname, city and daily results you choose to publish are sent to the Magmanet leaderboard server.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Online leaderboards (optional, off until you join)

Semantika can show public leaderboards for the daily puzzle (in your city, your region, Israel and the world). The first time you open them, the app asks whether you want to take part. If you choose "Not now", nothing is ever sent to the leaderboards.

If you join:

  • What is sent: a nickname you pick (2–16 characters), the city you pick from a list (for players outside Israel: the country you pick), the district (region) of that city, and for each daily puzzle you solve on its day: the number of guesses, hint points and the time it took, plus your current daily streak. To check results are plausible, the puzzle number and its language are sent too. Practice, archive and race games are never sent.
  • What is not sent: no email, no phone number, no name, no contacts, no location from GPS or the network, no advertising or device identifiers. The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse.
  • Who sees it: your nickname, city and scores appear in the public tables, for every Semantika player.
  • Where it goes: the Magmanet leaderboard server, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • How long: your profile and scores stay on the server until you delete them (see below).
  • Change or delete: Settings → Leaderboards lets you change your nickname or city, stop sending scores, or Delete my leaderboard data, which deletes your account and all your scores and your profile from the server at once. Uninstalling the app does not delete online data (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so delete it in the app first, or contact us (below) with your nickname and we will remove it.

Semantika is not directed at children, and the leaderboards are only for players who choose to join.

Internet use

Without the leaderboards, Semantika works entirely offline; the only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below. With the leaderboards joined, it also connects to the Magmanet leaderboard server, to send your results and load the tables. The Android "internet" permission is needed for that and for any network connection, including the direct local-network connection between phones in Play nearby.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Semantika sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent (no words you guess, and no leaderboard nickname or city).

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Notifications and reminders: to show the daily reminder you turn on (Android 13 and later ask first). The reminder is scheduled on your device. The app also uses vibration for the reminder notification, and runs at start-up (after the phone restarts) only to schedule your reminder again, because Android clears scheduled reminders on restart. No data leaves the device.
  • Wi-Fi / local network (Play nearby): only when you open Play nearby, to find and connect to friends' phones on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and location (Play nearby, optional): only if you turn on "also use Bluetooth" in Play nearby (it is off by default). Semantika then uses Google Nearby Connections to link phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth discovery on some versions, so the app asks for it then. Semantika never reads, stores or sends your location.

Play nearby: what is exchanged

When you play with friends nearby, these go directly between the phones in the game, and nowhere else: the display name and emoji avatar you choose, and the game state (guesses, scores, who found the word). Nothing goes to the internet or to us, and nothing is kept after the game except your own results on your phone.
