Privacy Policy — Facilitation Kit (מתודות להנחיה)

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Facilitation Kit is made by Magmanet. It helps facilitators choose methods and plan sessions. The short version: your plans stay on your phone. Nothing you write is sent anywhere unless you choose the optional AI feature with your own Anthropic key, and then it goes only to Anthropic.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive your plans, the content you write, your notes, your own methods, the AI scripts or your API key. The app has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising. We never sell or share your data.

Plans and methods on your phone

  • Plans (title, content, topic phrase, audience, group size, format, time, outcomes, the session arc, notes and any AI script) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete a plan at any time; uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Recommendations and session arcs are computed entirely on your phone from the built-in method library.
  • Your own methods and favorites are saved on your phone only.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your settings, plans and methods to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. Where you paste it is up to you. Your API key is never included.

Optional AI with your own Anthropic API key

The AI feature is off unless you add your own API key from Anthropic (console.anthropic.com). We do not provide, embed or proxy any key, and we never see yours.

  • Your key is stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via secure storage). It is sent only to api.anthropic.com, in the request header, and only when you ask for AI scripts. It is never logged, never included in usage statistics, plans or backups. You can delete it in Settings at any time.
  • When you tap "Write scripts" on a plan, the app sends the plan's title, content, topic phrase, audience type, group size, format, total time, familiarity, chosen outcomes and the chosen methods (names, steps and minutes) directly from your phone to Anthropic's Messages API over HTTPS, with instructions asking for ready-to-say scripts. The app asks you to confirm first (you can choose not to be asked again).
  • The request goes directly from your phone to Anthropic. It does not pass through any Magmanet server.
  • Anthropic processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and bills usage to your Anthropic account. The answer is shown to you and saved with the plan on your phone.

Sharing plans

When you tap Share (text or PDF), the app opens your phone's share sheet with the plan. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. The shared text includes a link to the app's page.

Internet use

Planning needs no internet. The app connects to the internet only for: (1) the optional AI requests to Anthropic described above, (2) the update check, and (3) the anonymous usage statistics.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google, Anthropic or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "built a session arc", "shared a PDF", or the type of an AI error such as "bad key") and for how long. It never includes plan titles, content, notes, method texts you wrote, AI scripts, your API key or anything you typed. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the optional AI requests, the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions.

Children

The app is intended for adults. It collects no personal information from anyone.
