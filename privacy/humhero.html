Privacy Policy — זמזום (Hum Hero)

Last updated: 2026-11-20

זמזום (Hum Hero) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your voice is never recorded, saved or sent, and your data stays on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

The microphone

  • The app uses the microphone only while it is listening on screen: during a level played in voice mode, while you calibrate your voice range (about 3 seconds per note), and while you hum a new level (up to 10 seconds, with a countdown and a live pitch trace).
  • It asks for the microphone permission the first time one of these starts. You can say no: the game then switches to touch mode (drag to set the pitch), and everything else still works.
  • The sound is analyzed live, in memory, as it arrives: which note you are singing and how loud. Each slice of about 23 milliseconds is reduced to a few numbers and then thrown away. No recording is ever written to your phone, kept, or sent anywhere.
  • When you hum a level, only the resulting notes (pitch steps and lengths, as numbers) are kept, so you can play the level again. Your voice itself is not.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. The app has no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your settings (language, sound, music, voice or touch mode, your voice range as two note numbers), your stars and best scores, and the levels you hum (notes, your rating, stars) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. You can delete hummed levels in the app and reset your progress in Settings.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sharing

  • Share code opens the system share sheet with a short text: a code that contains your hummed tune's notes and tempo (never audio) and a link to the app's page. You choose where it goes; the app never uploads it itself.
  • Share on a result shares the level name, your stars and accuracy, and the link.
  • Enter a code reads a code you paste into the app. Codes are decoded on the phone; nothing is looked up online.

Internet use

The game works offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: never audio, no measurements of your voice, no notes, no scores and no codes.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only when a voice level, the calibration or a hum recording starts (see above).
  • Internet: the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

No camera, contacts, location, Bluetooth or storage permissions.

Children

The app is made for a general audience (teens, adults and families) and is not directed at children under 13. It has no ads, no chat and no accounts, and it collects no personal information.
