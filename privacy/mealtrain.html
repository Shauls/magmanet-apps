Privacy Policy — Meal Train (ארוחות בתור)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Meal Train (ארוחות בתור) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your meal trains are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • The meal trains you create or join, because sharing is what the app does: the train's name, its purpose and dates, the slots (day and kind of help), and, if the organizer adds them, the address, drop-off notes, preferred drop-off time, kosher level, diet, allergies, kids' ages and household size. For each slot: who took it, what they wrote they will bring, when and a note; hearts and thank-you card texts; and reports of inappropriate content.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your trains.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. The address is free text that the organizer chooses to type. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a train

Only its members. A train starts with its organizer; others join only with the invite code or link, which a member chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the schedule, who took which slot and what they bring, the private details and the other members' nicknames. Before joining, someone holding the code sees only the train's name, its purpose and dates, the organizer's nickname and the number of members — never the address or the diet details. Nobody else can read a train: there are no public trains, no search and no profiles. The organizer can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. Anything written by others can be reported; after three reports it is hidden.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your trains (so they work offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your trains and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; slots show who took them by nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

While a train is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and the list of trains is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it (and when you pull down to refresh). Without a connection, your changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

Reminders

When you take a slot, the app can show a local notification the evening before (you choose the hour, or turn it off in Settings). Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself; no push service is used and nothing about them is sent to any server.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a train (train menu → Leave the train): you are removed from it and your slots become open again.
  • Delete a train (organizer): the train and everything in it is deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every train and your anonymous account is deleted from the server. Trains you organized pass to the next member.
  • Trains nobody has changed for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your trains on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave your trains or use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the train name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your trains. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the train's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. A thank-you card you share is an image made on your phone with a link to the same page. Both only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Meal Train sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a train was created or a slot was taken). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no train names, addresses, dishes or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your trains with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (and starting after a reboot): the local reminders the evening before your slots.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" or "Paste code".

Children

Meal Train is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.

Contact
