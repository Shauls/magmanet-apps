Privacy Policy — Quiz Builder (חידונית)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Quiz Builder is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your quizzes and pictures stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. The app has no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

The quizzes you write, the pictures you add to questions, your team names, your personal bests and your settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your quizzes, settings and bests (not the pictures) as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Pictures and the camera

  • Gallery: when you tap "Add a picture", the system picker opens and you choose one picture. The app keeps a smaller copy in its own private folder. It never looks at the rest of your gallery.
  • Camera: used only by the QR scanner, to read a shared quiz's QR code, and only while the scanner is open. Frames are read on the device and never recorded, saved or sent.

Sharing a quiz

You decide when to share. "Share as a file" hands the quiz (with its pictures) to the app you pick in your phone's share sheet; "QR code" shows the quiz (without pictures) on your screen for someone to scan; "Copy the quiz code" puts it on your clipboard. Nothing is uploaded by Quiz Builder itself.

Host mode: what is exchanged

When you host a quiz, these go directly between the phones in the room over the same Wi‑Fi (or, on Android, a direct Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi link if a player turns Bluetooth on), and nowhere else: the display name and emoji each player chooses, the question text and options of the current question, each player's answers, and the scores. Pictures are shown only on the host's screen. Nothing goes to the internet or to us, and nothing is kept on the players' phones after the game.

Internet use

Making and playing quizzes works entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below. The Android "internet" permission is needed for those and for any network connection, including the direct local-network connection between phones in host mode.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo). The request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (website/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate and stores no identifiers and no IP address. If the server is unavailable, the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: no quizzes, no questions, no answers, no pictures, no names.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Camera: only when you open the QR scanner.
  • Photos: the system photo picker; on older Android versions, access to the picture you choose.
  • Wi‑Fi / local network: only in host mode or when joining a quiz, to find and connect to the other phones on the same Wi‑Fi.
  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and location (optional, Android): only if a player turns on "Also use Bluetooth". Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth discovery on some versions. The app never reads, stores or sends your location.

Children

Quiz Builder is a general-audience app for the person who builds and hosts quizzes (teachers, parents, friends). It is not directed at children, though families and classes play the quizzes together.
