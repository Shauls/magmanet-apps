Privacy Policy — Bagrut Math (בגרות במתמטיקה)

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Bagrut Math is made by Magmanet. It helps students practise for the math bagrut. The short version: your answers and progress stay on your phone.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive your answers, scores, mistakes or anything else about your practice. The app has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising. We never sell or share your data.

Data on your phone

  • Progress (how many questions you answered per exercise type and how many were right, the list of questions to review, which worked examples you read, your best timed score and the days you practised) and settings (language and level) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. You can reset your progress in the app; uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Exercises are generated on your phone. Worked examples and formula sheets are built into the app.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your settings and progress to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. Where you paste it is up to you.

Sharing the app

When you tap "Share the app", your phone's share sheet opens with a short text and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

Practice needs no internet. The app connects to the internet only for (1) the update check and (2) the anonymous usage statistics.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "opened a worked example" or "finished a practice", with the number of correct answers in that practice) and for how long. It never includes the questions, your individual answers, or anything you typed. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions.

Children

The app is meant for high-school students and adults, not for children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
