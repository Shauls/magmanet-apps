Privacy Policy — Math Practice 1-6

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Math Practice 1-6 (תרגול חשבון א'-ו' / Exercices de maths) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Math Practice has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Each child's profile (the first name or nickname you type, an animal picture and a grade), their progress (level, right and wrong answers, stars and day streak per skill) and the settings (screen language, round length, pictures on or off) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app, and the grown-ups area can clear a child's progress or remove a child at any time.

The app's Copy backup feature (grown-ups area → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

Math Practice works entirely offline. The only time it goes online is the update check. Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). The check carries no identifiers and nothing about your child or your device. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and a grown-up (behind the parent gate) chooses whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in the grown-ups area ("Check for updates automatically").

Sharing

The grown-ups area has a "Share the app" button. It opens your device's share menu with a short text and the app's web address; you choose where (if anywhere) it goes. The app sends nothing by itself.

Permissions

Math Practice's only permission is internet access, used just for the daily update check described above. It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

Math Practice is designed for children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat or social features, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The settings, the progress by skill, the share button and the update link are behind a parent gate (a math question for grown-ups); there are no other links out of the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
