Privacy Policy — זיכרון קבוצתי (Group Memory)

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Group Memory is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your devices.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Group Memory has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Settings, the players' names for "take turns on one phone", and solo records are saved only on the device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The Copy backup feature (grown-ups area → Your data) copies this data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. The app never uploads it.

Playing together on several phones

When you play the giant board, the phones talk to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi network (or, only if a grown-up turns it on, over Bluetooth / Wi-Fi Direct). They exchange each player's display name and emoji avatar, and the game itself (which card was flipped, who found which pair). This goes only to the other phones in the same game; nothing is sent to us or to any server, and nothing is kept after the game.

Internet use

The game itself never needs the internet. The only time the app goes online is the update check. Once a day the app asks the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) whether a newer version exists. The request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play). The server counts checks per app and version in aggregate and stores no identifiers and no IP address. If the server is unavailable, the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice; the download link opens only after a grown-ups question. You can turn the check off in the grown-ups area.

Permissions

  • Internet and Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast: to find the other phones on the same Wi-Fi and connect to them, and for the update check.
  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and location (Android): only if a grown-up turns on "Also use Bluetooth" on the Play nearby screen. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth discovery on some versions; the app never reads, stores or sends your location.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

Group Memory is designed for children and families. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links out of the app (except the update download link, which is behind a grown-ups question), and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Settings are behind a grown-ups question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
