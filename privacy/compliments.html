Privacy Policy — Kudos (מחמאות)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Kudos (מחמאות) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: you use a nickname, kudos are always anonymous, and nobody – not your friends, not the group's creator – can see who sent a kudos. Kudos are made only of preset positive prompts: there is no free text and no chat. You need no account, e-mail or phone number. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

Kudos is for people aged 13 and older.

What is sent to the server, precisely

  • Your nickname (2–16 characters; no real name needed), shown to the members of the groups you are in.
  • The groups you create or join: the group's name, its type (friends, family, class or team) and its emoji.
  • The kudos you send: which built-in prompt (for example "Who always makes you laugh?") and which member of the group gets it. Nothing you type.
  • "Thank you" reactions you send back on kudos you received, and reports of a kudos.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. They never contain nicknames, group names, prompts or who got a kudos.

The app does not send your location, contacts, photos, e-mail address or phone number. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

How kudos stay anonymous

  • Every group is created with the server's anonymous setting, which can never be turned off. The server needs to know which member sent each kudos (so you can't report your own, and so leaving a group and deleting your data work), but no server answer ever contains the sender of a kudos or who said thank you – not to the receiver, not to other members, not to the group's creator.
  • Rounds start only when a group has at least 4 members, so a kudos never points at an obvious sender.
  • The receiver sees the prompt, the group and the day. The sender sees only that their kudos got a thank-you (as a number).

Who sees what

  • Members of a group (people who joined with its invite code) see the group's name, the members' nicknames, and the kudos in the group: which prompt went to which member, without the sender. The app itself shows you only your own kudos and the number of kudos given in the group this week.
  • The group's creator (or the oldest member after they leave) can also remove members, make a new invite code or delete the group. The creator sees no senders.
  • Nobody outside a group can see it.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: your nickname and settings, a copy of your groups (so they open offline), kudos waiting to be sent when you were offline, and your kudos inbox (the kudos you received, with their prompt, group name and day; it stays on the phone even after you leave a group). Members you hid from your rounds and how many rounds you played today are kept only on the phone. All of this is removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies it to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: the data listed above under "What is sent", run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other users never see these ids; inside a group members are numbered 1, 2, 3…

Safety

  • Only preset positive prompts; no free text, no chat, no images.
  • Hide a member from your rounds (on your phone), report a kudos (3 reports hide it from the group), report a group name (you leave the group), and leave any group at any time.
  • The group's creator can remove members and change the invite code.
  • Each group allows up to 3 rounds a day on your phone.

How long, and how to delete

  • The app deletes the kudos you sent from a group after 35 days (receivers keep them in their inbox on their phones).
  • Leave a group (group menu): you are removed, and your thank-you reactions there are deleted. The kudos you received stay in your inbox on your phone; kudos you sent stay in the group, still without your name.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account and its data are erased from the server, and your inbox and badges are erased from the phone. Kudos you already sent may stay in their groups (until the group is deleted); they never showed you as the sender and are linked only to a group member number that is never reused.
  • Groups untouched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your data on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below).

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server for your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

When you share an invite or your "my week" card, the text (or image) is created on your phone. An invite contains the group name, the invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The card contains your number of kudos this week, your flame and your top prompts – no names. It only goes where you send it.

Anonymous usage statistics

Kudos sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a kudos was sent or a card shared). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no nicknames, group names, prompts or receivers.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: your groups on the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or notifications.

Children

Kudos is not directed at children under 13; the app asks users to confirm they are 13 or older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
