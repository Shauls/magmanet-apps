Privacy Policy — Building Committee (ועד בית)

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Building Committee (ועד בית) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a building's records are stored on the Magmanet server so that its members can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; receipt photos stay on your phone; the app never moves money; and it also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

Because sharing a building's records is what the app does, these are stored on the Magmanet server:

  • The building: its name, an address if the committee enters one, the fee schedule, the opening balance and the invite code.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed) and your role, shown to the members of the building, and which flat is yours if you say so or the committee sets it.
  • Flats: the number, and a name on the flat and a note if the committee enters them. All members see these.
  • Recorded payments: which flat paid what, for which month or collection, on what date, how, and a note. The committee sees all of them. A resident sees only their own flat's payments and the building's totals, unless the committee chooses to show everyone who has paid.
  • Expenses, collections, repair requests and notices, as written by the committee and residents, and who wrote them by member number. All members see these.
  • A change log of recorded, changed and deleted expenses, removed or changed payments (the amount only, not the flat), changes to the fee and opening balance, and changes of committee role. All members see it.

Please enter only what your neighbours should see. The app doesn't ask for phone numbers, ID numbers or bank details, and there is no need to type them.

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids.

What stays on your phone

  • Receipt photos. A photo you take or choose for an expense is saved in the app's private storage and is never uploaded. The server only knows that a receipt exists. If you share one, you do it yourself through your phone's share sheet.
  • A copy of the building's records, changes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The app is excluded from device backups. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this copy to your clipboard as text, including the sign-in token of your anonymous account; where you paste it is up to you, so keep it private. It doesn't include photos.

Money

The app only keeps records. It holds no bank or card details, doesn't connect to any bank or payment service, and no money passes through it.

Sharing

Invite codes, payment reminders, notices and balance reports are shared only when you tap Share, through your phone's share sheet (for example to WhatsApp). The text is also copied to your clipboard. The app keeps no phone numbers and sends no messages by itself.

Where the data is stored

On the Magmanet server, hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The server keeps no access log with your IP address for these requests. People who aren't members of a building can't see it, and it isn't copied to our test systems.

How the app talks to the server

While a building is open the app asks the server for changes about every 30 seconds, and when you open the app, come back to it or pull down to refresh. New notices appear when you open the app; there are no push notifications. Without a connection, what you record waits on the phone and is sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leaving a building removes your nickname and membership from it and deletes its copy and your receipts from your phone. Records you wrote stay with the building without your name. The last committee member hands over the role before leaving.
  • Settings → Delete all my data leaves every building and deletes your anonymous account. Where you were the only committee member, the longest-standing resident takes over the role. A building nobody is left in is deleted.
  • The committee can delete a building for everyone.
  • Buildings nobody has opened for 2 years are deleted. Deleted records are removed from the server after 30 days and change-log lines after 3 years.
  • Uninstalling removes everything on your phone but not the building on the server; use the delete option first.

If you ask us to delete something a committee recorded about you, we may refer you to the committee, which manages the building's records.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics to the Magmanet server: a random install ID, the app name and version, your Android or iOS version, on Android the phone's manufacturer and model, the language, how the app was installed, which screens and features are used and for how long, counts (such as how many flats a new building has), and daily counts of error types. Never what anyone typed: no names, amounts, addresses, notices or codes.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

On Android the app also checks once a day whether a newer version is available.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing the building with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (and starting after a reboot, to keep the reminder): only if you turn on the monthly reminder in Settings. It is a local notification made on the phone and says nothing about any flat or amount.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to add a receipt. On Android the system's camera and photo picker are used and the app holds no camera or storage permission; on iOS the system asks you the first time.

The app has no access to your contacts, microphone or location.

Children

Building Committee is meant for adults who run or live in a building, and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
