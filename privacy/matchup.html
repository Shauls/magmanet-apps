Privacy Policy — Match Up (מתאימים?)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Match Up (מתאימים?) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a decision (its cards and everyone's swipes) is stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can swipe it too, and only they can see it; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your decisions, because deciding together is what the app does: the question, the deck, the cards (ready cards are stored by a short key; cards you write are stored as their text and emoji), and each member's swipe on each card (like, pass, super-like or veto).
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your decisions.
  • Reports of offensive custom cards (which card was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that card.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a decision

Only its members. A decision starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the question, the cards, the other members' nicknames, how many finished swiping, and who liked which card. With private swipes turned on when the decision is created, nobody sees who liked what, only the totals. Nobody else can read a decision: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your decisions (so they show offline), swipes waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your decisions and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a decision is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds (for the live progress), and your list of decisions is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your swipes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a decision (decision menu → Leave decision): you are removed from it and your swipes are deleted. When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a decision (creator): the decision, its cards and all swipes are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every decision, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname is removed from the phone.
  • Decisions nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your decisions on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the decision's question and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your decisions. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the decision's question, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The result card you share is a picture made on your phone. Both only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Match Up sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no questions, cards, swipes or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your decisions with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy code".

Children

Match Up is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
