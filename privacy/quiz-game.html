Privacy Policy — Quiz Game

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Quiz Game is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Quiz Game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

Quiz Game works entirely offline. The only time it goes online is to check, at most once a day, whether a newer version of the app is available: it downloads a small public version file from GitHub Pages (shauls.github.io). Nothing about you or your device is sent (no personal data, no identifiers); like any web request, GitHub can see your device's IP address. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
