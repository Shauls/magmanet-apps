Privacy Policy — Bucket List together (רשימת חלומות)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Bucket List together (רשימת חלומות) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: shared bucket lists are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see them, and only they can; solo lists and photos never leave your phone; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your shared lists, because sharing is what the app does: the list name and emoji, the kind of list (couple, family, friends), and each dream's title, category, target season or year and cost level.
  • "Who's in" answers: which members tapped "I'm in" or "Maybe" on a dream.
  • Done marks: when someone marks a dream done, the date, the short note they write and their nickname. Everyone on the list sees them.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your lists.
  • Reports: when a member reports a dream, the server records the report so that a dream reported by several members can be hidden.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Solo lists stay on your phone

A solo list ("Just me") is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It works without internet and is never sent to the server. If you choose "Share this list", its dreams and done notes are copied to a new shared list on the server (the photos stay on your phone) and the solo copy is removed.

Photos stay on your phone

A memory photo is picked with the system photo picker, copied into the app's private folder on your phone and shown only on your phone. It is never uploaded to the server or sent to anyone. Other members see that a dream is done and the note, not your photo. Removing the photo, deleting the dream or the list, or "Delete everything" deletes it, and so does uninstalling the app.

Who can see your shared lists

Only the members of each list. A list starts with you; others join only with the list's invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the list, its dreams, who's in, the done notes and the other members' nicknames. Any member can edit a dream or mark it done; only the person who added a dream, or the list owner, can delete it. Nobody else can read a list: there are no public lists, no search and no profiles. The owner can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: your solo lists, a copy of your shared lists (so they work offline), changes waiting to be sent, your photos, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies the settings and the local copy of your lists to your clipboard as text (not the photos); where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your shared lists and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a shared list is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and the list overview is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it (and when you pull down to refresh). Without a connection, your changes wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave a list (list menu → Leave list): you are removed from it and your "who's in" answers are removed. The dreams you added stay on the list.
  • Delete a list (owner): the list and all its dreams are deleted from the server for everyone. A solo list is deleted from the phone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): the lists you own are deleted for everyone, you leave every other list, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your solo lists, photos and settings are removed from the phone.
  • Lists nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted dreams are kept for up to 30 days so that syncing works, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your shared lists on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the list name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync shared lists. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The messages you share are created on your phone and only go where you send them: the invite (list name, invite code and link, and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site), "ask a friend" and spinner messages (a dream's title and the app's page), and the year-in-review card (an image of the card with the list name, the dreams done that year and their notes).

Anonymous usage statistics

Bucket List together sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a list was created or a dream was marked done). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no list names, dreams, notes or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing shared lists with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Photos (only when you tap "Add a photo"): the system photo picker gives the app the one photo you choose.

The app has no access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste".

Children

Bucket List together is a general-audience app. Families can make a list together; the app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
