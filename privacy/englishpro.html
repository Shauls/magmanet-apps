Privacy Policy — English for Adults (אנגלית למבוגרים)

Last updated: 2026-11-14

English for Adults is made by Magmanet. It teaches everyday English to adults. The short version: your progress stays on your phone. The app has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising, and we never sell or share your data.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive what you say in speaking practice, what you type into email templates, or your learning progress.

Data on your phone

  • Progress (which phrases and cards you have practiced and when they are due again, your streak, XP, completed dialogues, your level and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Email templates: what you type into the fill-in fields stays on the screen only and is not saved. When you tap Copy, the email is copied to your clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your progress and settings to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it.

Listening and speaking

  • Text-to-speech: the app reads English aloud using your device's built-in speech engine. Nothing is recorded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only after you tap the mic in a speaking exercise or role-play, so the app can compare what you said with the target phrase. Your speech is turned into text by your device's own speech-recognition service (on Android usually Google's, on iPhone Apple's, which may process it online under their own privacy policies). The app compares the text with the phrase on your phone and keeps nothing: no recordings or transcripts are stored or sent to us. Speaking practice is optional; you can turn it off in Settings.

Sharing

"Share the app" in Settings opens your phone's share sheet with a short message and a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

Learning needs no internet. The app connects to the internet only for (1) the update check and (2) the anonymous usage statistics. (Speech recognition, if you use it, is handled by your device's service as described above.)

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "finished the daily lesson", "copied an email template") and for how long. It never includes what you said, what you typed, or which phrases you studied. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Microphone (record audio): only for speaking practice, only after you tap the mic.
  • Speech recognition (iPhone): to turn your speech into text for speaking practice.

The app requests no location, camera, contacts or notification permissions.

Children

The app is intended for adults. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
