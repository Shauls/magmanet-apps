Privacy Policy — PlayCord

Last updated: 2026-10-18

PlayCord is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything runs on your device. PlayCord collects nothing.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. PlayCord has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

The songs you open are analysed on your phone. The analyses (chords, beats, sections, your edits), your settings, setlists, favourites, pasted lyrics, recordings you make and your practice scores are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Your audio files are never uploaded.

When you share or save something (a chord chart, a PDF, a MIDI file, a practice track), the app hands it to the app or folder you choose. Nothing is sent unless you do this, and where it goes is up to you.

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste lyrics", and copies to it only when you tap a copy button.

Internet use

PlayCord works offline. Its only internet request is the update check: at most once a day, release versions of the app download a small public version file from GitHub Pages (shauls.github.io) to see whether a newer version is available. Nothing about you or your device is sent; like any web request, GitHub can see your device's IP address. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link (or the store page, if you installed the app from the store). Development (debug) builds skip this check.

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Microphone: only while you use live chord recognition, the tuner, the chord-change drill or "Record". The sound is analysed on the device. A recording you make is saved on the device only; nothing is uploaded.
  • Internet: only for the daily update check described above.

Children

PlayCord is a music tool for teens and adults and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
