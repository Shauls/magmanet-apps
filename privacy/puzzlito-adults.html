Privacy Policy — Puzzlito

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Puzzlito (the logic-puzzle edition, package com.magmanet.puzzlito_adults) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Puzzlito has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your puzzles, progress, scores and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

Puzzlito doesn't connect to any internet server. It uses the network only for Nearby race, to talk to other phones on your local network (see below). All puzzles are generated on your device.

Nearby race (local multiplayer)

Nearby race (Play nearby) lets you race friends whose phones are in the same room. Phones connect directly to each other over your local Wi-Fi network or hotspot or, only if you turn on the Bluetooth switch in the lobby (off by default), over Bluetooth using Google's Nearby Connections. Nothing goes through the internet or to us.

  • What the other players see: the display name and emoji you choose, plus game state: whether you're ready, the puzzle and difficulty being raced, your progress and your finish time. There is no chat.
  • Wi-Fi permissions: the app declares the internet and Wi-Fi permissions only to find and connect to the other phones on the same network.
  • Bluetooth and location permissions: on some Android versions, Android requires the location permission (and, on newer versions, the "Nearby devices" permission) before an app may search for nearby Bluetooth devices. Puzzlito asks for these only when you turn the Bluetooth switch on. The app never reads, stores or sends your location. If you say no, Nearby race still works over Wi-Fi.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
