Privacy Policy — Hebrew Calendar (לוח שנה עברי)

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Hebrew Calendar is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. Hebrew Calendar has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Your settings (language, city, holiday schedule, candle-lighting and havdalah choices, reminder) and your Shabbat checklist are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Location

The app does not use your phone's location. You choose your city from a built-in list; all dates and times are calculated on the phone from that city's coordinates.

Sharing a countdown

When you tap "Share", the app opens your phone's share sheet with a picture of the countdown card and a short text (the number of days, the holiday's name and date, and a link to the app's page). It contains nothing about you. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Internet use

The calendar, zmanim and checklist work entirely offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

Hebrew Calendar sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long — never your city, your checklist items or anything you type). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and the anonymous usage statistics described above.
  • Notifications (asked only if you turn on the Shabbat reminder, which is off by default), run at startup (to keep the reminder after a restart) and vibration (for the reminder). Reminders are created on the phone; nothing is sent.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Accuracy

Times are calculated astronomically and are approximate. For halachic decisions, follow your rabbi and your local community calendar.

Children

Hebrew Calendar is a general-audience app. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
