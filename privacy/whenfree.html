Privacy Policy — When Free (מתי נפגשים?)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

When Free (מתי נפגשים?) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your events are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can see them, and only they can; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your events, because finding a date together is what the app does: the event name, type, place and length, the proposed dates and times, each member's answer to each date (yes, if needed or no), polls and votes, and the date the organizer locked.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your events.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts, your calendar or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see your events

Only the members of each event. An event starts with its organizer; others join only with the event's invite link or code, which someone in the event chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the event, its dates and polls and the other members' nicknames and answers. If the organizer turned on "Hide who answered what", members see only the totals for each date and poll, not who answered what. Nobody else can read an event: there are no public events, no search and no profiles. The organizer can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. Every date and poll written by someone else has a "Report" action; after several reports it is hidden.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your events (so they work offline), answers waiting to be sent, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your events and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While an event is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of events is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, your answers wait on the phone and are sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Leave an event (event menu → Leave event): you are removed from it and your answers and votes are deleted. If you were the organizer, the longest-standing member takes over; when the last member leaves, the event is deleted.
  • Delete an event (organizer): the event, its dates, answers and polls are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every event and your anonymous account is deleted from the server.
  • Events nobody has changed for 400 days are deleted automatically; deleted dates and polls are kept for up to 30 days so that syncing works, then removed.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your events on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so leave your events or use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the event name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Holidays and the calendar file

Shabbat and Israeli holidays are worked out on your phone from the Hebrew calendar; nothing is sent for this. "Add to calendar" creates a standard calendar file (.ics) on your phone and hands it to the share sheet, where you choose your calendar app. The app never reads or changes your calendar.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your events. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message you share contains the event name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The final card and message contain the event name, date, time and place. They are created on your phone and only go where you send them.

Anonymous usage statistics

When Free sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that an event was created or a date was locked). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no event names, dates, answers or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your events with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen.

Children

When Free is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
