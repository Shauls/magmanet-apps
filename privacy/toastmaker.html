Privacy Policy — Toast & Speech Maker (נאום וברכה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Toast & Speech Maker is made by Magmanet. It helps you write and practice toasts, speeches and blessings. The short version: your speeches stay on your phone. Nothing you write is sent anywhere unless you share it yourself, or you choose the optional AI polish with your own Anthropic key, and then it goes only to Anthropic.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. We never receive your speeches, names, memories, codes or your API key. The app has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising. We never sell or share your data.

Speeches on your phone

  • Speeches (the occasion, the names you enter, your relation, memories, traits, tone, length, the built speech and your edits) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete a speech at any time; uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Speeches are built entirely on your phone from the app's built-in library of original lines.
  • Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies your settings and speeches to your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. Your API key is never included.

Collecting memories from friends

"Collect memories from friends" creates a code that contains only what you typed: the name of the person you celebrate, the occasion, your name if you entered it, and grammatical genders. Your friend's answer code contains only what they typed: their name, a memory and chosen traits. Codes are plain text; they never pass through any Magmanet server. They are sent only when you press Share and pick an app yourself. The app never reads your contacts and never sends anything automatically. "Paste from clipboard" reads the clipboard only when you tap it.

Sharing a card

When you tap Share on the card screen, the app draws the card on your phone and opens the system share sheet with the image and a short text that includes a link to the app's page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Optional AI polish with your own Anthropic API key

AI polish is off by default. It works only if you turn it on in Settings and add your own API key from Anthropic (console.anthropic.com). We do not provide, embed or proxy any key, and we never see yours.

  • Your key is stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via secure storage). It is sent only to api.anthropic.com, in the request header, and only when you ask for a polish. It is never logged, never included in usage statistics, speeches or backups. You can delete it in Settings at any time.
  • When you tap "Polish with AI" and confirm, the app sends the speech text (including the names and memories in it), the occasion, the tone and the grammatical genders directly from your phone to Anthropic's Messages API over HTTPS, with instructions to polish the speech without inventing facts.
  • The request goes directly from your phone to Anthropic. It does not pass through any Magmanet server.
  • Anthropic processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and bills usage to your Anthropic account. The answer is shown to you and saved with the speech on your phone; you can undo it.

Internet use

Writing and practicing need no internet. The app connects to the internet only for: (1) the optional AI polish described above, (2) the update check, and (3) the anonymous usage statistics.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google, Anthropic or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone manufacturer and model (never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed, a random install ID, and anonymous usage events: which screens and features are used (for example "built a speech", "shared a card", "opened a code", or the type of an AI error such as "bad key") and for how long. It never includes names, memories, speech text, codes, your API key or anything you typed. The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the optional AI polish, the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience writing speeches for family events. It collects no personal information from anyone.
