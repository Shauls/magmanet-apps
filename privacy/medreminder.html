Privacy Policy — Meds on Time (תרופות בזמן)

Last updated: 2026-10-31 · App: com.magmanet.medreminder · Developer: Magmanet

English

Meds on Time reminds you and your family to take medications and keeps a simple record of what was taken. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • People and medications you enter (names, a picture emoji, medication names, doses, times, weekdays, notes and pill counts) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • The dose log (taken / skipped, and when it was marked) is stored only on your phone and kept for 180 days.
  • Notifications. If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled locally on your phone. The notification permission is asked only when you turn them on.

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no server for your data.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used and simple counters, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include names, medications, doses or history. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share", the daily summary text goes only to the app and person you choose.
  • Backup. "Copy backup" in Settings copies your data as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

Not medical advice

The app is a personal reminder and checklist. It does not give medical advice and does not check doses or drug interactions. Always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions.

Deleting your data

Delete a medication or a person in the app (their history is deleted too). Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
