Privacy Policy — Pet Salon

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Pet Salon (המספרה של החיות / Salon des animaux) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. Pet Salon has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no usage statistics. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Game progress (stars, unlocked animals and accessories, salon decorations and the photo album) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app. The in-game "photos" are drawings made by the app and kept inside the app; the app never uses the camera and never saves to your gallery.

The app's Copy backup feature (grown-ups area → Your data) copies your progress as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. The app never uploads it.

Internet use

Pet Salon works entirely offline. The only time it goes online is the update check. Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). The check carries no identifiers and nothing about your child or your device. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice, and the download button is behind the parent gate. You can turn this off in the grown-ups area ("Check for updates automatically").

Permissions

Pet Salon's only permission is internet access, used just for the daily update check described above. It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

Pet Salon is designed for children aged 5–10. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app (except the update download link, which is behind a parent gate) and no chat or social features, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Settings are behind a parent gate (press and hold for 3 seconds).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
