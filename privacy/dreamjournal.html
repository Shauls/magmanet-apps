Privacy Policy — Nightleaf (יומן הלילה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · App: com.magmanet.dreamjournal · Developer: Magmanet

English

Nightleaf is a private dream journal. What you write in it stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your dreams: the text, title, mood, lucid and recurring marks, clarity and sleep ratings, tags for people, places and themes, and your reflections. They are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Settings: language, look, the reminder time and whether a PIN is on.
  • The PIN, if you set one, is never stored itself: only a salted hash, in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Nobody can recover a forgotten PIN.

The dream text itself, and everything else you write, is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no server for your journal.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (which screens are used and simple counters such as "a dream was saved", with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include dream text, titles, tags, moods or reflections. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check (Android) asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Dictation (optional). If you tap Dictate, the app uses your phone's own speech recognition service (from Google on Android, Apple on iOS). Depending on the phone, that service may process the audio on its provider's servers under the provider's privacy policy. The app explains this before the first use, and you can always type instead.
  • Backup. "Export a backup" creates a JSON file and opens the share sheet; where you save or send it is up to you. The file is not encrypted, and the app warns you before exporting.

Not interpretation, not medical advice

The app records what you write and counts it. It does not interpret dreams. The tips in the Guide are general information, not medical advice.

Deleting your data

Delete a dream in the app, or use Settings → "Delete everything" to remove every dream, the PIN, the reminder and the settings from the phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored. Backups you exported are outside the app's reach.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
