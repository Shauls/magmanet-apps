Privacy Policy — What's the Price? (כמה זה עולה?)

Last updated: 2026-11-25

What's the Price? is made by Magmanet. The short version: your games stay on your device. The app asks our server for supermarket prices (sending only product barcodes) and sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

By default, only anonymous usage statistics (see below). There is no sign-up, no account, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data. If you choose to join the online leaderboards, the nickname, city, optional profile and daily results described below are sent to the Magmanet leaderboard server.

Where your data is stored

Your games, guesses, scores, streak, the saved price snapshots and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset games and stats. Copy backup (Settings → Your data) copies them as text to your clipboard; the app never uploads it.

Online leaderboards and player profile (optional, off until you join)

What's the Price? can show public leaderboards for its daily puzzle (in your city, your region, Israel and the world). The app asks whether you want to take part (the first time you open the leaderboard, and from time to time after a game). If you choose "Not now" or "Later", nothing is sent to the leaderboards.

  • What is sent: a nickname you pick (2–16 characters), the city you pick from a list (for players outside Israel: the country you pick), the district (region) of that city, and the result of each daily puzzle you finish on its day (the score and its tiebreak, such as the time or the number of moves), plus the numbers the server needs to check that a result is plausible.
  • Who sees it: your nickname, city and scores appear in the public tables, for every What's the Price? player.
  • Player profile and friends (optional): you can also pick an avatar (an emoji on a color) and type your full name. Your avatar, level, number of games, streaks and the trophies you earned are shown with your nickname. Your full name is shown only to players you add as friends, never in the public tables. You add a friend by exchanging an 8-character friend code; friends see each other's nickname, full name, avatar, city, level, trophies and the day each last played, and appear together on a Friends table. You can remove a friend, get a new friend code, or clear your full name at any time, and Delete my leaderboard data also deletes your profile and your friends list.
  • Where it goes: the Magmanet leaderboard server, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • How you are identified: an anonymous account created by the server. No email, no phone number, no GPS.
  • Change or delete: the leaderboard settings let you change your nickname, city, avatar or full name, stop sending scores, or Delete my leaderboard data, which deletes your account, profile, friends list and all your scores from the server at once. Uninstalling the app does not delete online data (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so delete it in the app first, or contact us (below) with your nickname and we will remove it.

Games played, streaks and trophies are also counted on your phone when you have not joined; they stay on the phone until you join.

Prices

To build each day's game the app asks the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) for the prices of that day's products: GET /v1/basket/item/<barcode> with a fixed area (Tel Aviv-Yafo). The request contains only the product's barcode and that fixed area. No location, account or identifier is sent, and the app has no location permission. The prices come from the price files Israeli supermarket chains publish by law; they may differ in store.

Update check

Once a day the app asks the Magmanet server whether a newer version exists (app name, version and how it was installed only; no identifiers, no IP address stored). If the server is unavailable it reads the public version file from GitHub Pages. You can turn this off in Settings.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party: app name and version, Android version, phone manufacturer and model, language, how the app was installed, a random install ID made by the app, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used, for how long, and each finished game's score). On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored; raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count users and improve the app. It is on by default; turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"), which stops sending and deletes the random ID from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: prices, the update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, notification or sensor permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
