Privacy Policy — El HaMaayan Yoga Studio

Last updated: 2026-10-06

El HaMaayan Yoga Studio is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect anything. El HaMaayan Yoga Studio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies your data as text to your clipboard, so you can keep it somewhere you choose. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Internet use

El HaMaayan Yoga Studio works entirely offline. The only time it goes online is to check, at most once a day, whether a newer version of the app is available: it downloads a small public version file from GitHub Pages (shauls.github.io). Nothing about you or your device is sent (no personal data, no identifiers); like any web request, GitHub can see your device's IP address. If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you turn on. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Opening links: if you choose to open a link, it opens in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp, SMS and email: when you tap one of these, the app opens that app with the message and recipient filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Your students' information

The app is a tool for running a studio, so you may enter information about other people: your students' names, phone numbers, emails, birthdays, emergency contacts, health-declaration status and health notes, attendance, payments and leads. It is stored only on your device, like everything else; we never receive it. As the studio you are responsible for that information under privacy law (for example, Israel's Privacy Protection Law): collect only what you need, tell students you keep it, and use the app's PIN lock and backups sensibly. Deleting a student in the app deletes their records from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
