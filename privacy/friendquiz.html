Privacy Policy — כמה אתם מכירים אותי? (Know Me Quiz)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

כמה אתם מכירים אותי? (Know Me Quiz) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your quizzes and your friends' answers never go to a server. They travel only inside the messages you choose to send, and they are kept only on your phone. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off. The app has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage: your settings (language, sounds, the work-safe default), the name and face you last used, the quizzes you made, the results your friends sent you, and the quizzes you answered. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or with "Delete all quizzes and results" in Settings.

The app's Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this data as text to your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you; the app never uploads it.

Sending quizzes and results

When you tap a send or share button, the app opens your phone's share menu with a message (and, for the picture options, an image) that contains a code. The quiz code holds your quiz: your name, the face you picked, the questions and your answers. The result code holds the friend's name, their answers and score. You choose who gets it and through which app; that app's own privacy terms apply to the message. Nothing is sent automatically, the app never reads your contacts and never asks for phone numbers.

When you paste a code, the app reads it on your phone only. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste".

Internet use

The quiz works offline. The only internet requests are the update check and the anonymous usage statistics below.

Once a day the app checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent: no names, no questions, no answers, no scores, no codes.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

Only the internet permission (for the update check and the statistics). No camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions.

Children

The app is made for a general audience (13 and up) and is not directed at children under 13. It has no ads, no chat and no accounts, and it collects no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
