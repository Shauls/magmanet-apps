Privacy Policy — Lunch Order (מזמינים צהריים)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Lunch Order (מזמינים צהריים) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a group's menus and orders are stored on the Magmanet server so that the people you invite can order together, and only they can see them; you only need a nickname; there are no payments in the app; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your groups, because ordering together is what the app does: the group's name and type (office, class, family…), the places you order from (name, and a phone number and delivery fee if someone typed them), the menus typed by members (dish names, prices, sizes), the orders (place, closing time, delivery fee and how it is split), each member's dishes (size, quantity, notes and allergy or diet tags they chose to add), "where do we order?" polls and votes, and who is marked as paid.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your groups.
  • Reports of offensive content (which item was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that item.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, your contacts, your location or any payment details. "Paid" is only a mark that people set by hand; no money moves through the app. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Allergy and diet tags are optional. Add them only if you want the organizer and the restaurant to know; everyone in the group can see them next to your dish.

Who can see a group

Only its members. A group starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the group's places, menus, orders, everyone's dishes with their notes and tags, the totals, who is marked as paid, polls and who voted for what, and each other's nicknames. Nobody else can read a group: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they show offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname, your usual allergy tags (to fill in the next dish) and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your groups and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google, to restaurants or to any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of groups is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, what you add waits on the phone and is sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete a dish, a menu item or a place you added: it is deleted for everyone.
  • Leave a group (group menu → Leave the group): you are removed from it and your votes and "I paid" marks are deleted; dishes you added stay in the group's order history. When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a group (creator): the group, its menus, orders and polls are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname and allergy tags are removed from the phone.
  • Groups nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the group's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message, the nudge for people who haven't ordered, the poll message and the list for the restaurant are made on your phone. They only go where you send them, through the app you choose.

Anonymous usage statistics

Lunch Order sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no group names, menus, dishes, notes, allergy tags or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy" (the invite or the list for the restaurant).

Children

Lunch Order is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
