Privacy Policy — Turn Clock (שעון התור)

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · App: com.magmanet.gameclock · Developer: Magmanet

English

Turn Clock is a chess clock and a turn timer for board games. It works offline and has no account.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your own time controls, the player names you type for a board game, the last table set-up, an unfinished game (so you can continue it), and settings (language, sounds, low-time warning, keeping the screen on) are stored only on your device.

Nothing of this is sent to us. On Android it is also excluded from Google's automatic app backup. On iPhone, a backup of the whole device that you make to iCloud or a computer may include the app's data, like that of other apps.

What leaves the device

  • What you share. When you share a game summary or the app, the text goes only to the app, person or place you choose in the share sheet.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens and features used, simple counters, and counts of error types, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include names, times or time controls. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check (Android) asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Copy backup in Settings copies your data as text to the clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

The app shows no ads, has no purchases, sends no notifications, uses no microphone, camera or location, and opens no links of its own.

Deleting your data

Delete your time controls in the app and discard an unfinished game from the home screen. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is for a general audience and collects no personal information from anyone: the only data it sends is the anonymous statistics described above, which can be turned off.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
