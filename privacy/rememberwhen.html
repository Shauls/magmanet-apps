Privacy Policy — Remember When (זוכרים ש...?)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Remember When (זוכרים ש...?) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a group's memories, nicknames and quiz scores are stored on the Magmanet server so that the friends you invite can see them, and only they can; your photos never leave your phone unless you send one yourself; you only need a nickname; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your groups, because remembering together is what the app does: the group's name and type (classmates, army, youth movement, college, cousins, other), the year it began, and the reunion date, time and place if the creator sets one.
  • Nicknames (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of the group.
  • Memories members add: what happened, the year and optionally the day, the place, who was there (members of the group, or first names of friends who aren't in the app yet, as typed by the member), a quote and who said it. Not the photo.
  • "My version" notes members write on a memory, emoji reactions, and reports of offensive memories (which memory was reported, by which anonymous account, used only to hide it).
  • Daily quiz scores: for each member, the points and right answers of recent days, the total and the number of days played, shown on the group's leaderboard.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

Photos are never uploaded. A photo you take or pick for a memory is copied into the app's private folder on your phone and stays there; each member can keep their own photo for a memory on their own phone. The memory card and the yearbook PDF are made on your phone (the PDF includes the photos on your phone) and only go where you send them.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see a group

Only its members. A group starts with its creator; others join only with its invite code or link, which a member chooses to send (for example on WhatsApp). Members see the group's memories, the member list, everyone's versions, reactions and quiz scores. Nobody else can read a group: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working. Any member can report an offensive memory; after a few reports it is hidden.

When you tag a friend who isn't in the app, only the first name you type is stored, and only the members of the group see it. The "who else should be here?" invite is a message you send yourself through your phone's share sheet; the app never contacts anyone.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your groups (so they show offline), changes waiting to be sent, your photos, your nickname and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your groups and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

How the app talks to the server

There are no notifications. While a group is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of groups is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, what you add waits on the phone and goes out when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete a memory (your own, or any memory if you created the group): it is deleted from the server for everyone, and its photo from your phone.
  • Remove "my version" on a memory: it is deleted from the server.
  • Leave a group (The gang tab → Leave the group): you are removed from it, your reactions and versions are deleted and its photos are deleted from your phone. The memories you added stay in the group for the others.
  • Delete a group (creator): the group and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete all my data (Settings): you leave every group, your anonymous account is deleted from the server (DELETE /v1/rooms/me), and your nickname and photos are removed from the phone.
  • Groups nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the photos and the local copy, but not your groups on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete all my data" first, or contact us (below) with the group's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your groups. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite messages you share contain the group's name, its invite code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site. The reunion card's "When Free" button opens that app's page on the Magmanet apps site in your browser; nothing about your group is sent with it.

Anonymous usage statistics

Remember When sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no groups, memories, names or photos.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your groups with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Camera and photos (only when you tap Camera or Gallery on a memory): the system camera or photo picker opens and the one photo you choose is copied into the app's private folder. On iPhone the app asks for camera / photo library access at that moment.

The app has no access to your contacts, microphone or location, and sends no notifications. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy code".

Children

Remember When is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.
