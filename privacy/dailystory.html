Privacy Policy — Daily Story (סיפור יומי משותף)

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Daily Story (סיפור יומי משותף) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the sentences you write are public – that is the game – and are shown with your pen name (or as "anonymous writer"); you need no account, e-mail or phone number; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your sentences. Each day you may add one sentence (up to 140 characters) to the day's story in Hebrew or English, and sentences to the stories you write with friends.
  • Your pen name, if you choose one (2–16 characters; no real name needed). Without one, your sentences are signed "anonymous writer". In stories with friends you choose a name the other members see.
  • Your reactions (❤️ 😂 😮) and reports of sentences.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an e-mail address, a phone number, a name, your contacts or your location. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

What is public

  • The daily story is public. While a day's story is being written, other writers see only its last two lines (your sentence may be one of them, with your pen name). At midnight (Israel time) the whole story is published in the app: every visible sentence with its pen name and reaction counts, and the best sentence of the day. Published stories stay in the app's archive.
  • Stories with friends are private to the members who joined with the invite code; while a story is open each member sees only its last sentence, and everyone sees the whole story when it ends.
  • Reactions are shown only as counts; nobody sees who reacted. Reports are seen only by the moderator.
  • Please don't write personal details about yourself or anyone else. Sentences with links, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, @handles or offensive words are refused by the server.

Moderation

To keep the story friendly the server checks every sentence against a word filter (Hebrew and English), limits how often an account and a network address can write and report, and lets readers report a sentence. A sentence reported by several readers is hidden until Magmanet reviews it; Magmanet may hide sentences and block an anonymous account from writing. For this, reported sentences and the anonymous account id that wrote them are visible to the moderator.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of today's slice and of finished stories (so the archive works offline), a sentence waiting to be sent when you wrote it offline, which sentences are yours and which you reacted to or reported, your pen name and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app. The Copy backup feature (Settings → Your data) copies this to your clipboard as text; where you paste it is up to you.
  • On the Magmanet server: your sentences (with your pen name and the time), the stories with friends you are in, your reactions and reports, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The server keeps no access log with your IP address for these requests; the address is used in memory only, to limit abuse.

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends once when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other users never see these ids.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete my sentences (Settings): your daily sentences, reactions and reports are deleted from the server, you leave every story with friends (your sentences there stay, without your name, so your friends' story keeps its shape) and your anonymous account is deleted.
  • Leave a story with friends (story menu): you are removed from it; your sentences stay in it. When the last member leaves, the story is deleted.
  • Published daily stories are kept as the app's archive until you delete your sentences. Stories with friends nobody has touched for a year are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your sentences on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete my sentences" first, or contact us (below) with the date and the sentence and we will remove it.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server for the daily story and stories with friends. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

When you share a story or an invite code, the text (or image) is created on your phone and contains the story, the code and a link to the app's page on the Magmanet apps site (GitHub Pages). It only goes where you send it.

Anonymous usage statistics

Daily Story sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long, for example that a sentence was sent or a story shared). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no sentences, story titles or pen names.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: the daily story and stories with friends on the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.

The app has no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and sends no notifications.

Children

Daily Story is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It shows text written by other users; it is filtered and can be reported, but it is not reviewed before it appears.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
