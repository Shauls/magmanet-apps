Privacy Policy — Watch Diary (יומן צפייה)

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · App: com.magmanet.watchlist · Developer: Magmanet

English

Watch Diary is a personal tracker for the series and movies you watch. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • What you enter (titles, seasons and episodes, where you watch, statuses, ratings, notes, which episodes you watched and when, rewatch counts and reminder times) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No online catalogue. The app does not look titles up on the internet and downloads no posters or descriptions. The pictures next to each title are drawn by the app from the name.
  • Notifications. If you set a weekly "new episode day" reminder, it is scheduled locally on your phone. Its text (the title's name) can appear on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings.

What leaves the phone

  • Anonymous usage statistics (screens used and simple counters, with a random install ID) help us improve the app. They never include titles, ratings, notes or your watch history. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • The daily update check asks our website whether a newer version exists.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share" on a title, its name, kind, platform, your stars and notes go only to the app you choose.
  • Backup file. If you create one, a JSON file with your list and history goes to the app you choose (for example your own cloud drive or email). It is not encrypted. It is not sent to us. "Copy backup" in Settings copies the same data as text to your clipboard.

On Android, the phone's automatic cloud backup and device transfer are turned off for this app. On iPhone, a backup of the whole phone (iCloud or computer) can include the app's data under Apple's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Delete a title to remove it with its history. Restoring a backup replaces the list on the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is meant for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data? Open an issue at https://github.com/Shauls/magmanet-apps/issues
