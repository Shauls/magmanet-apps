Privacy Policy — ShareBox (משאילים בבניין)

Last updated: 2026-11-21

ShareBox (משאילים בבניין) is made by Magmanet. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: a box's things, borrow requests and wanted posts are stored on the Magmanet server so that the neighbors you invite can lend to each other, and only they can see them; you only need a nickname; photos stay on your phone; there is no money in the app; the app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

What we collect

  • Your boxes, because lending to each other is what the app does: the box's name and type (building, street, kibbutz, friends…), the things members list (name, icon, category, condition, the most days it is lent for, whether a deposit is asked and a short note about it, the pickup place typed by the owner, such as "Apartment 12", and an optional note), borrow requests (which thing, the dates, a short note), the owner's answers (approved, handed over, returned, with an optional short reply and the time), "I returned it" marks, wanted posts (what, by when, a note) and "I have one" offers with an optional short note.
  • Your nickname (2–16 characters, chosen by you; no real name needed), shown to the members of your boxes.
  • Reports of offensive content (which item was reported, by which anonymous account), used only to hide that item.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (see below), which you can turn off.

The app does not ask for an email address, a phone number, a name, an address, your contacts, your location or any payment details. There are no payments and no rentals: lending is free. A deposit, if an owner asks for one, is agreed in person and never goes through the app. There is no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your data.

The pickup place is free text you choose to type. Type only what you are comfortable sharing with the members of that box (an apartment number is usually enough).

Who can see a box

Only its members. A box starts with you; others join only with its invite code or link, which you choose to send (for example in your building's chat). Members see the box's things with their rules and pickup places, all borrow requests and their answers (so the building can see what is out, with whom and until when), wanted posts and offers, the lending counts of each member (lent, borrowed, returned on time), and each other's nicknames. Before joining, an invite shows only the box's name, the creator's nickname and the number of members. Nobody else can read a box: there is no public content, no search and no profiles. The creator can remove a member and make a new invite code so the old one stops working.

Photos

A photo you add to a thing is copied into the app's private folder on your phone and never uploaded. Neighbors see the thing's icon, not the photo. Photos are deleted when you take the thing off the box, use "Delete everything" or uninstall the app.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: a copy of your boxes (so they show offline), changes waiting to be sent, your nickname, your usual pickup place, photos of your things, the list of scheduled reminders and settings, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • On the Magmanet server: your boxes and your nickname in them, run by Magmanet on a server hosted in the European Union (by Contabo). It is not sent to Google or to any other third party and is not used for advertising or profiling. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

The account is anonymous: the server creates a random user id and access tokens, stored on your phone. The app also stores a random number made at install time (not a device identifier), which it sends when the account is created so the server can limit abuse. Other members never see these ids; they see your nickname only.

Reminders

Due-back reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your phone from the dates of your loans (the evening before and the morning a thing is due back; for owners, the evening it is due). Nothing is sent to a push service. You can turn them off in Settings ("Due-back reminders") or in your phone's settings.

How the app talks to the server

While a box is open the app asks the server for changes every few seconds, and your list of boxes is refreshed when you open the app or come back to it. Without a connection, what you add waits on the phone and is sent when you are back online.

How long, and how to delete

  • Delete a wanted post you wrote: it is deleted for everyone. Take a thing off the box: it disappears from the box; if it was ever lent, its record stays in the box so the lending counts stay right.
  • Cancel a request you made: it is marked cancelled.
  • Leave a box (box menu → Leave the box): you are removed from it and your answers and offers are deleted; things, requests and posts you added stay in the box's history. When the creator leaves, the oldest member takes over.
  • Delete a box (creator): the box and everything in it are deleted from the server for everyone.
  • Delete everything (Settings): you leave every box, your anonymous account is deleted from the server, and your nickname, pickup place, photos and reminders are removed from the phone.
  • Boxes nobody has touched for 400 days are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your boxes on the server (the anonymous account can't be reached any more), so use "Delete everything" first, or contact us (below) with the box's name and your nickname and we will remove them.

Internet use

The app connects to the Magmanet server to sync your boxes. Once a day it also checks for a newer version by asking the Magmanet server (EU, Contabo) — the request contains only the app name, its version number and how it was installed (site/Google Play); the server counts checks per app and version in aggregate, stores no identifiers and no IP address; if the server is unavailable the app downloads the public version file from GitHub Pages instead (GitHub may see your IP address). If a newer version exists, the app shows a notice and you choose whether to open the download link. You can turn this off in Settings ("Check for updates automatically").

The invite message, the join card image and the wanted post message are made on your phone. They only go where you send them, through the app you choose.

Anonymous usage statistics

ShareBox sends anonymous usage statistics over HTTPS (a short ping once a day; usage events a few times a day at most, batched) to Magmanet's own server, hosted in the EU (Contabo), not to Google or any other third party. It sends the app name and version, your Android version, your phone's manufacturer and model (such as Samsung SM-S911B; never the name you gave the phone), your language, how the app was installed (from Google Play, the website or elsewhere), a random install ID, and anonymous usage events (which screens and features are used and for how long). The random ID is made by the app when it is installed; it is not a device identifier. No personal content is sent with the statistics: no box names, things, requests, posts, pickup places or nicknames.

On the server the ID is hashed and no IP address is stored. Raw data is deleted after 400 days. It is used only to count active users and improve the app, never for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics"). Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes the random ID and any unsent usage events from your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet: syncing your boxes with the Magmanet server, the daily update check and the anonymous usage statistics.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer asks you): the due-back reminders. Run at startup: to keep scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Photos (only when you tap "Add a photo"): the system photo picker opens and the photo you pick is copied into the app's private folder.

The app has no access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the join screen, and written only when you tap "Copy the invite".

Children

ShareBox is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Changes
