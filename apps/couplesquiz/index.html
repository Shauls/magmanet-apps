How well do you two really know each other?

Couples Quiz is a game for two: each of you answers the same questions about yourself, then guesses what the other one answered. What would you order at an Italian restaurant? Ideal vacation? Who of us falls asleep first during a movie? At the end you get a compatibility score, a "knows you" score for each of you and a fun title, from "Full of surprises" to "Soulmates".

💞 10 packs, 220 questions:

  • First dates
  • Living together
  • Long-term love
  • Family & future
  • Travel
  • Food
  • Fun & silly
  • Deep talk
  • Best friends edition
  • Parent & kid edition

📱 Three ways to play:

  • One phone: pass it over; answers stay hidden until the end.
  • Two phones: side by side over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, no internet.
  • Send a challenge: answer now and send a short code on WhatsApp. The other side pastes it, answers, and sends the result back. No server, no sign-up.

📤 A designed result card to share as an image.

Questions work for every couple, any pairing, and for best friends and parents with kids. Kind, no sexual content, suitable for ages 13 and up. Hebrew and English, no ads.
